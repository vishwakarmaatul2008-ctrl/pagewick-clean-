# Chapter 4 – The One-Year Deal

"Don't worry. I will not make you do this for free," Grandmother said.

Elena's eyes widened.

"What?"

Grandmother smiled.

"I will invest in your business until it becomes successful. And all you have to do is stay in a relationship with him."

Elena stared at her.

"This isn't right."

"Why?"

"It would be unfair to take your money and then walk away."

Grandmother raised an eyebrow.

"So, if you want to make it fair, then become a good girlfriend."

Elena became even more confused.

A relationship.

For one whole year.

With a man she had met only tonight.

And in return, Grandmother would invest in her pastry business.

She didn't know what to think.

Her mind was already full of problems.

She had to make forty cupcakes for a church event the next morning.

She still had to pay the rent for her apartment.

And now this.

Elena rubbed her forehead.

"I don't know..."

Grandmother laughed.

"Come on. You have the whole night, my Cinderella."

Elena looked at her.

Grandmother continued teasingly,

"And don't worry. Your godmother's magic won't finish at twelve o'clock."

Elena couldn't help herself.

She started laughing.

Grandmother laughed with her.

After a moment, Grandmother pointed toward the sofa.

"Sit here and think calmly. I'll come back in two minutes."

Elena nodded and sat down.

Grandmother walked away.

But instead of leaving the ballroom, she went to find Damien.

---

Damien was standing near one of the pillars, looking irritated and confused.

Grandmother approached him.

"Damien."

He looked at her.

"What now?"

"I have a deal for you."

Damien sighed.

"What kind of deal?"

Grandmother looked at him seriously.

"You only have to stay in a relationship with Elena for one year."

Damien stared at her.

"One year?"

"Yes."

"And after that?"

"It will be your choice."

Damien remained silent.

Grandmother continued.

"You can decide what you want to do with her and your future. If you want to stay with her, stay. If you want to break up with her, break up."

Damien looked at his grandmother.

"And you?"

"I won't force you again."

Damien's expression changed slightly.

Grandmother continued,

"No more girls. No more parties. No more meetings arranged for you. No more pressure about getting engaged."

Damien thought for a moment.

Then he sighed.

"Stop hunting girls for now and take care of yourself first."

Grandmother smiled.

"So?"

Damien looked toward Elena.

"Okay."

He paused.

"I'll stay in a relationship with her for one year."

Grandmother's eyes suddenly filled with tears.

Damien noticed.

"Grandmother?"

She looked down.

"I know it hurts you."

Damien became serious.

"But I want to have a family around you."

Her voice trembled.

"I can die at any moment because of my age."

Damien's expression immediately softened.

He stepped forward and hugged her.

"Don't say this type of thing again."

Grandmother hugged him tightly.

"Okay."

She wiped her tears.

"I won't."

They stayed like that for a moment.

Then Damien pulled away.

He looked toward Elena.

"I'll not talk to her about it."

Grandmother looked confused.

"What?"

"I've already decided."

He nodded toward Elena.

"Tell her yes from my side."

Grandmother stared at him.

"What are you talking about?"

Damien looked at her.

"It is the last time."

Grandmother smiled.

"Okay. Fine."

---

Meanwhile, Elena was still surrounded by Vivienne, her group of friends, and Damien's friends.

They were all curious about her.

One of the girls looked at Elena.

"Are you an actress?"

Elena shook her head.

"No."

"A model?"

"No."

"Then what do you do?"

Elena hesitated.

"I sell cupcakes."

For a moment, everyone was silent.

Then someone laughed.

"Cupcakes?"

Elena nodded.

"Yes."

Another girl looked around.

"But I don't see any cupcakes at the party."

A few people laughed again.

Elena felt uncomfortable.

She didn't know what to say.

One of Damien's friends asked,

"Then how did you get the invitation?"

Elena answered honestly.

"Grandmother invited me."

"Why?"

Elena looked toward the direction Grandmother had gone.

"She invited me after I rescued her from the pool."

Everyone suddenly became quiet.

"You rescued her?"

Elena nodded.

"She fell into the pool."

The group exchanged shocked looks.

Before anyone could ask another question, Grandmother suddenly appeared among them.

"Elena."

Elena turned toward her.

Grandmother smiled.

"I think you are very lucky."

Elena looked confused.

"Why?"

"Because Damien also likes you."

Elena's eyes widened.

What do you mean by 'also like you'?

Vivienne immediately interrupted.

"Did Damien say that he told you yes?"

Grandmother looked at her.

"Yes."

Vivienne's expression changed.

Grandmother continued,

"He is a little shy, and that's why he sent me."

Elena's eyes widened even more.

Grandmother smiled.

"But don't worry. They will get to know each other very well."

She placed a hand gently on Elena's shoulder.

"Elena is going to stay in the mansion with Damien."

Everyone became even more shocked.

"Really?"

"She is staying here?"

Grandmother smiled.

"Yes."

Then she raised her voice.

"My grandson has chosen Elena as his girlfriend from now on!"

Cheers immediately erupted around the ballroom.

Elena stood frozen.

She hadn't even agreed yet.

She looked around helplessly.

Damien's friends were congratulating him.

The guests were smiling.

People were whispering excitedly.

But Vivienne's face was completely different.

She was furious.

One of her friends leaned toward her.

"Vivienne..."

"What?"

"I saw what happened near the pool."

Vivienne slowly turned toward her.

"You did?"

Her friend nodded.

Vivienne's eyes narrowed.

"Tell me everything."

---

Not long afterward, Damien and Elena were sitting together.

Neither of them was looking at the other.

Neither seemed to be enjoying the party.

Elena kept looking down at her hands.

Damien glanced at her.

After a few seconds, he asked,

"Do you want a drink?"

Elena shook her head.

"No."

"You don't drink?"

She shook her head again.

"No."

Damien nodded.

"Okay."

Silence returned.

Suddenly Vivienne appeared.

"Damien."

He looked up.

"I want to talk with you alone for a few minutes."

Damien looked at Elena.

"Can I go for a few minutes?"

Elena nodded while looking down.

"Yes."

Damien stood up and followed Vivienne.

---

They walked to a quieter corner of the ballroom.

Vivienne immediately turned toward him.

"Why are you taking permission from her?"

Damien frowned.

"What?"

"You asked her if you could leave."

"So?"

Vivienne smiled.

"It means you really love her."

Damien's expression became serious.

"No."

"Then why are you behaving like that?"

"My grandmother told me she's a nice girl."

Vivienne folded her arms.

"I think this girl is hiding something."

Damien looked at her.

"What?"

"How can she win your grandmother's heart that easily?"

Damien didn't answer.

Vivienne continued.

"I think she's the one behind the pool accident."

Damien's eyes widened.

"What?"

"Yes."

Vivienne lowered her voice.

"The girls near the pool were my friends. They told me what happened."

Damien listened carefully.

"They said she was the one who bumped into your grandmother."

Damien frowned.

"And?"

"When the other girls tried to stop her, she used a knife from her old tote bag."

Damien's expression became serious.

"A knife?"

"Yes."

Vivienne continued.

"She scared them, and they ran away to call security."

She paused.

"And that's when she got the chance to manipulate your grandmother."

Damien stared at her.

"She knew she couldn't manipulate you."

Vivienne looked toward Elena.

"But your grandmother is an easy target."

Damien remained silent.

Vivienne continued,

"And how did she even enter the backyard?"

Damien frowned.

"She said my grandmother invited her."

"But she didn't have an invitation."

Damien's expression changed.

Two things were beginning to bother him.

The story about the pool.

And the question of how Elena had entered the mansion.

Vivienne watched his face carefully.

Damien finally said,

"I'll find out later."

He turned around.

"I'm going back to her."

Vivienne smiled faintly.

"Of course."

Damien walked away.

But his expression had changed.

He was no longer simply curious about Elena.

Now he had doubts.

And he didn't know which version of the story was true.

---

Damien returned to Elena.

She was still sitting quietly.

He sat beside her.

For a few moments, he simply stared at her.

Elena could feel his eyes on her.

She became nervous.

Finally, Damien spoke.

"Why are you not looking at me or talking with me?"

Elena's fingers tightened around her dress.

"I'm feeling tired and sleepy."

She finally looked at him.

Damien stared directly into her eyes.

Elena quickly looked down again.

Suddenly, Damien gently held her face with one hand.

"Don't worry."

Elena's eyes widened.

"After the party, we'll sleep together."

Elena's entire face turned red.

She quickly removed his hand from her face and looked down again.

Damien watched her reaction carefully.

His expression became serious.

Don't worry.

I'll find out who you are.

And what is your purpose?
