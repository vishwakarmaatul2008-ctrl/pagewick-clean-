# Chapter 2 – The Girl Who Came for a Job

The Moretti mansion was already preparing for the evening's grand party.

But among the countless guests, expensive cars, and beautifully dressed women heading toward the estate, there was one young woman who had absolutely no idea what was happening inside.

Her name was Elena Rossi.

At twenty-three, Elena had a quiet, warm personality that made people feel comfortable around her. She was patient, helpful, and naturally kind, the sort of person who would remember a regular customer's favorite flavor without being asked.

And she was beautiful.

Elena had long, dark-brown hair that fell in soft waves down her back, warm hazel-brown eyes, and gentle features that gave her an approachable appearance. A tiny beauty mark rested just beneath her left eye. She didn't wear much makeup, mostly because she couldn't afford to spend money on things she didn't consider necessary.

Her beauty had never been something she relied on.

Her hands were more important.

Those hands had been making cupcakes since she was old enough to understand that money could buy food.

Elena sold cupcakes and pastries from a small street stall, and occasionally received larger orders from sweet shops, birthday parties, and private events.

It wasn't glamorous.

But it was hers.

She lived alone in a tiny apartment that she had managed to pay for herself, one month at a time.

That morning, Elena had received a phone call.

She had been hired.

Not permanently, but it was a start.

The position was for a maid at the Moretti estate. If she worked well, there was a possibility of becoming a permanent member of the household staff.

The salary was far better than what she usually earned in a week.

Elena had accepted immediately.

That evening, she stood in front of the small mirror in her apartment, trying to decide what to wear.

She didn't own a fancy dress.

After several minutes, she finally chose the nicest outfit she had.

A simple sleeveless top and a short skirt.

Casual.

Clean.

Presentable.

She looked at herself in the mirror and smiled.

"Good enough."

She picked up her tote bag and placed her printed job application inside it, just in case anyone asked for proof that she had actually been hired.

Then she left.

---

The bus was crowded.

Elena found an empty seat near the window and sat down, holding her tote bag against her lap.

She watched the city pass by.

She had no idea that by the end of the night, her ordinary life would be completely different.

When the bus finally reached her stop, Elena stepped off and looked toward the distant Moretti estate.

It was still a considerable walk.

She could take a cab.

She checked her wallet.

Then she shook her head.

"Nope."

A cab was definitely outside her budget.

So she started walking.

Twenty minutes later, Elena finally reached the street leading toward the Moretti mansion.

And stopped.

Her eyes widened.

"What...?"

The entire street looked different.

Luxury cars were moving toward the enormous estate one after another.

Men in expensive suits stepped out of them.

Women wearing elegant gowns and sparkling jewelry followed.

The entrance to the mansion was decorated with flowers and warm golden lights.

Elena slowly looked down at herself.

Then back at the mansion.

"Why does a maid's workplace look like a wedding venue?"

She laughed nervously.

Maybe rich people simply liked making everything dramatic.

She continued toward the entrance.

The closer she got, the more confused she became.

There were security guards everywhere.

Guests were being welcomed through the main entrance.

Elena stopped beside the gate and opened her tote bag.

She pulled out her application.

She checked it.

Nothing about a party.

She pulled out her phone and checked the message she'd received about the job.

Still nothing.

"Maybe they forgot to tell me."

Before she could think any further, a security guard approached her.

"Excuse me, ma'am."

Elena turned.

"Yes?"

"Where are you going?"

"I was hired for a job."

The guard looked at her suspiciously.

"What kind of job?"

"I'm supposed to work here."

"As what?"

Elena blinked.

"As a maid."

The guard exchanged a glance with another security officer.

"Did you apply for a company position?"

"No."

Elena opened her tote bag and pulled out the printed application.

"Here."

The guards examined it.

After a few moments, one of them nodded.

"Alright. You can enter."

Elena smiled with relief.

"Thank you."

"But not through the front entrance."

She looked toward the grand entrance.

"The front area is for guests. Staff enter from the backyard."

"Oh."

Elena nodded.

"Okay."

She walked toward the side entrance.

Still confused.

Very confused.

---

The backyard of the Moretti mansion was almost as impressive as the front.

Flowers covered the pathways.

Soft lights hung from the trees.

There were tables filled with food and drinks.

And at the center was an enormous swimming pool surrounded by elegant decorations.

Elena walked slowly, taking everything in.

She had never seen anything like it.

Then she heard laughter.

A group of young women were gathered near the pool.

Elena glanced toward them.

They were dressed beautifully.

Designer dresses.

High heels.

Expensive jewelry.

Their outfits looked as if they were attending a wedding.

Elena frowned.

Why are all these guests here?

Then she noticed an elderly woman standing nearby.

She was elegantly dressed, with silver-white hair and an expression that suggested she was accustomed to having everyone listen when she spoke.

Elena didn't know her.

But that woman was Beatrice Moretti.

The grandmother of Damien Moretti.

Beatrice was surrounded by the young women, who were laughing, teasing one another, and trying desperately to impress her.

Elena stayed at a distance.

She didn't want to interrupt.

Then one of the girls stepped backward.

She bumped directly into Beatrice.

"Watch where you're going!"

Beatrice lost her balance.

Her eyes widened.

And then---

Splash!

She disappeared into the pool.

For a second, everyone froze.

Then Beatrice surfaced.

"Help!"

The girls stared at her.

One of them looked horrified.

Another looked at her expensive dress.

"You pushed her!"

"Go save her!"

"Absolutely not!" the girl snapped. "My dress and makeup will be ruined."

"But she's drowning!"

"Call security."

The girls began backing away.

"I don't want Damien to see me looking like this."

Then they left.

All of them.

Beatrice struggled in the water.

"Help!"

Elena's eyes widened.

She dropped her tote bag.

"Wait!"

Without thinking, she jumped into the pool.

The cold water shocked her.

She immediately grabbed Beatrice around the waist.

"Don't worry! I've got you!"

But Elena wasn't a strong swimmer.

Pulling another adult through the water was far harder than she expected.

"Come on..."

Her arms began to ache.

She kicked desperately against the water.

"Please..."

Finally, she reached the edge.

With the help of the pool's edge, Elena managed to pull Beatrice out.

Both women collapsed onto the ground.

Elena immediately wrapped her arms around the older woman.

"It's okay."

She was breathing heavily.

"Calm down. We're safe now."

Beatrice was coughing.

Then slowly, her breathing steadied.

She looked at Elena.

Really looked at her.

"Who... are you?"

"Elena."

Beatrice blinked.

"What is your name?"

"Elena Rossi."

Beatrice stared at her soaked clothes and wet hair.

Then suddenly smiled.

"Are you a mermaid?"

Elena froze.

"What?"

"A mermaid."

"No!"

Beatrice laughed.

"Because if you're a mermaid, I want to drown one more time so you can save me again."

Elena couldn't help laughing.

"You're unbelievable."

Beatrice studied her face.

"You're beautiful."

Elena looked away awkwardly.

"Thank you."

"And brave."

"Anyone would have helped."

Beatrice gave her a look.

"Apparently not."

Elena glanced toward the direction the other girls had disappeared.

She frowned.

"Those girls were cowards."

Beatrice's expression changed.

"They think they can get my Damien."

Elena looked at her.

"Your Damien?"

"My grandson."

"Oh."

Beatrice smiled proudly.

"Today he is supposed to choose his fiancée."

Elena's eyes widened.

"Wait..."

She looked around at the mansion.

The expensive cars.

The dresses.

The decorations.

The young women.

Suddenly everything made sense.

"So this is that party?"

"Yes."

Elena stared at her.

"You're holding a Moretti family member in your arms."

Beatrice laughed.

"Yes."

Then she suddenly became serious.

"What about your parents? They must have received an invitation too."

Elena's smile faded.

"No."

"Why not?"

"I don't have parents."

Beatrice's expression softened.

"What happened?"

"They died when I was ten."

Beatrice looked at her quietly.

"An illness," Elena continued. "Their treatment was expensive, and we couldn't afford it."

She swallowed.

"After they died, my relatives didn't want the responsibility of raising me. They left me behind."

Beatrice's face hardened.

"And you've managed alone ever since?"

Elena nodded.

"What do you do?"

"I sell cupcakes."

Beatrice stared at her.

Then laughed.

"You're a businesswoman too."

Elena smiled.

"Maybe a very small one."

"Small businesses can become big."

Before Elena could answer, someone suddenly grabbed her arm.

"Miss!"

Elena turned.

A woman in a formal uniform stood there.

The head maid.

She immediately bowed toward Beatrice.

"Madam, I'm terribly sorry. She has only been hired today. She wasn't supposed to be in the guest area."

Elena looked embarrassed.

"I was told to enter from the backyard."

The head maid sighed.

"I'll take her back to the staff area."

Beatrice looked at Elena.

"Wait."

The head maid stopped.

Elena smiled awkwardly.

"I told you I'm not a mermaid."

Beatrice raised an eyebrow.

"I know."

Elena pointed at herself.

"But I am a maid."

Beatrice suddenly burst into laughter.

"No."

Elena blinked.

"No?"

"You're not going to be a maid."

"What?"

Beatrice stood up, still dripping wet.

Then she looked Elena up and down.

"You saved my life."

Elena shook her head.

"I just helped."

"You also insulted the richest girls in my mansion without even knowing who I was."

"I... did?"

Beatrice smiled.

"I like you."

Elena stared at her.

"That's nice, but I still need the job."

"You have a better job now."

"What job?"

Beatrice's eyes sparkled.

"You're going to my party."

Elena's mouth fell open.

"I'm sorry?"

"Go get her ready."

The head maid looked shocked.

"Madam---"

"Find her something beautiful."

Elena looked between them.

"But I'm a maid."

Beatrice smiled.

"Not tonight."

She turned toward the mansion.

"Tonight, Elena Rossi..."

She paused.

"...you're going to meet my grandson."
