# A Night of Love, A Seed of Hatred (Enhanced Romance)

The wedding of Prince Caelan and Princess Seraphina was a celebration the kingdom would remember for generations.

Golden lights filled the streets.

Music echoed through the night.

Laughter, joy, and hope returned to Aurelion.

For once… the kingdom felt whole again.

But behind the beauty of celebration—

Darkness watched in silence.

## The King Who Did Not Celebrate

In a quiet chamber, away from music and joy, King Varen sat alone.

Wine filled his cup.

And anger filled his heart.

"Like father… like son," he muttered.

His grip tightened around the glass.

"That man took everything from me…"

His voice dropped, colder now.

"And now his son… stands where I should have stood."

The glass shattered in his hand.

Yet moments later—

Before the people, before the court—

He smiled like a loving king.

## A Question That Changed Something

Later that night, as the palace fell quiet, Caelan walked toward his chamber.

His heart was steady… but restless.

Then—

"Well then… how does it feel?"

Varen stepped forward, smiling faintly.

"To finally have a wife?"

Caelan looked slightly embarrassed.

"It's not like that."

Varen laughed softly.

"Of course."

Then Caelan asked—

"Are you… truly happy?"

For a brief moment—

The king's eyes darkened.

But then he stepped forward, pulling Caelan into a tight embrace.

"I am very happy… my dear nephew."

His grip tightened just a little too long.

Then he let go.

And walked away.

## The First Night

The chamber was quiet.

Soft candlelight danced along the walls.

Seraphina sat by the edge of the bed, her presence calm… yet breathtaking.

When Caelan entered—

He paused.

For a moment, he simply looked at her.

Then slowly…

He removed his royal cloak.

And then—

His upper garments.

Revealing the body shaped by years of battle.

Scars that told stories.

Strength that needed no words.

Seraphina's breath caught slightly.

Not out of shock—

But something deeper.

Something she had never felt before.

## Closer Than Words

"Come," she said softly.

Caelan sat beside her.

But the distance didn't last.

His hand moved to her waist—gentle, yet certain.

He pulled her closer.

She gasped softly, her cheeks warming as her hands instinctively rested against his shoulders.

"You're… beautiful," he said quietly.

Her eyes lowered, shy.

"Look at me," he whispered.

She did.

And in that moment—

Nothing else existed.

## The Truth of the Heart

"Why did you choose me?" Caelan asked.

His voice softer than ever before.

"Tell me the truth."

Seraphina hesitated… then smiled faintly.

"Because when you stood before me…"

Her fingers lightly traced his arm.

"I forgot how to speak."

Caelan smiled.

"And now?" he asked.

She leaned closer.

"I still do."

## A Moment That Became Forever

He pulled her gently into his arms.

Closer.

Warmer.

Real.

She rested against him, her heartbeat quickening.

"I've never felt this way before," he said quietly.

"Neither have I," she whispered.

His hand lifted her chin slightly.

And he pressed a soft kiss to her cheek.

Then another.

Closer.

Slower.

Until the distance between them disappeared.

That night was not rushed.

Not forced.

But built in quiet moments.

In glances.

In touches.

In trust.

Two hearts… choosing each other.

## The One Who Watched

Outside the door—

A shadow stood still.

Listening.

King Varen.

Every soft word.

Every breath.

Every moment of closeness.

His jaw tightened.

His eyes burned.

"He has everything…" he whispered.

"And I…"

Silence.

Then a cold, dangerous smile.

"…will take it from him."

## Morning Light

Sunlight filled the room.

Seraphina rested against Caelan, her head on his chest.

His arm wrapped around her protectively.

Peace.

For the first time in years—

Peace.

"I should go," he murmured.

She held him tighter.

"Stay."

Her voice was soft… but certain.

"I feel safe with you."

Caelan smiled.

"Then I won't leave."

## A King's Humiliation

But in the royal court—

The king waited.

And waited.

By the time Caelan arrived—

The silence had turned heavy.

Varen smirked.

"So," he said loudly, "How was your first night?"

The court froze.

Caelan's expression hardened.

"My apologies for being late."

But Varen continued—

"A strong man, it seems… takes all night."

A few uneasy glances spread.

Caelan stepped forward.

"My wife is not a subject for discussion."

His voice was calm.

But firm.

"Our life is not entertainment."

And without another word—

He turned.

And walked away.

## The Beginning of War

For the first time—

He did not obey.

For the first time—

He resisted.

And for the first time—

The king saw the truth.

Not a boy.

Not a weapon.

But a man.

A rival.

Love had begun.

But so had something far more dangerous.
