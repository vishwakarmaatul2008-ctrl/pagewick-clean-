# Chapter 2: The Mall Plan

By 2:08 PM the following afternoon, five people were waiting outside a shop at Phoenix Mall.

The sixth was Rishi.

**Meera:** He's late.

**Kabir:** Shocking. Nobody could have predicted this.

**Sana:** We should report him missing.

**Dev:** I'll make a poster. Last seen running away from responsibility.

Aarav checked his phone.

**Aarav:** He said he's parking.

**Meera:** He doesn't even drive.

**Aarav:** He said his brother dropped him.

**Meera:** Then his brother is late too. A family tradition.

Rishi finally appeared, walking toward them with a grin and a cold drink in his hand.

"Relax. It's only eight minutes."

"You're twenty minutes late," Sana replied.

"Time is a social construct."

"So is friendship," Meera said. "We're leaving you here."

Rishi put a hand over his heart. "After everything we've been through?"

"We've been through twenty minutes of waiting," Dev said.

They entered the mall together.

The air-conditioning hit them immediately. Music played from the stores, children ran between families, and shoppers moved through the bright corridors carrying bags. The place was crowded, but not unusually so for a weekend afternoon.

For a while, the previous night's conversation became another joke.

Kabir pointed at a security guard.

"That guy would be the first to die in a zombie movie."

Sana glanced at him. "You're judging a professional while wearing shoes with untied laces."

Kabir looked down.

"That's tactical flexibility."

"That's how you break your neck on the stairs," Meera replied.

They passed an electronics store. A television mounted above the display shelves showed a news bulletin.

The headline caught Aarav's attention.

**UNUSUAL VIOLENT INCIDENT REPORTED AT CITY HOSPITAL; POLICE INVESTIGATING**

The reporter spoke about an incident involving several people who had attacked hospital staff and other patients. The details were unclear. Authorities were asking the public not to spread unverified information.

Aarav slowed down.

"Wait."

"What?" Rishi asked.

"Look at the television."

Kabir glanced at it. "Some hospital fight. Happens every day."

"Not like this."

The footage changed to a shaky phone recording. A man in a hospital gown stumbled through a corridor. Several people were shouting behind the camera. The video ended just as the man lunged at someone.

The screen returned to the news anchor.

Meera frowned. "That's disturbing."

"Probably drugs," Kabir said. "Or some fake viral video."

Sana was already looking at her phone.

"There's more."

She opened a social media post. It showed another blurry video, apparently recorded outside a hospital. Someone in the comments claimed the person in the footage had bitten a nurse. Another person called it a movie promotion. Someone else said the video was from another country.

Nobody seemed to know anything for certain.

"Could be fake," Sana said. "But the news report is real."

Aarav felt an uncomfortable pressure in his chest.

He wanted to tell them that the timing was strange. He wanted to explain that the videos might be connected.

Instead, he said, "Let's not get involved. We don't know what's happening."

"Look at that," Meera said. "The zombie professor is finally using his brain."

They continued toward the food court.

The smell of fried food and coffee filled the air. Every table seemed occupied, and people stood in queues, arguing over orders and calling friends to find seats.

Rishi bought food for himself and then stole fries from everyone else's trays.

"You're a criminal," Dev told him.

"An opportunistic criminal."

"One day, your greed will destroy you."

"Then avenge me."

They laughed.

For the next twenty minutes, the news report faded into the background. They discussed movies, college, and a ridiculous video Dev had edited the night before.

At 2:31 PM, a scream rose from the floor below.

It was loud enough to cut through the music.

People stopped talking. Several shoppers leaned over the railing.

Then another scream followed.

A man came running up the escalator, pushing past people as he climbed. His shirt was soaked with blood, and he kept looking behind him.

"Move!" he shouted. "Get away!"

A woman appeared at the bottom of the escalator.

She was moving strangely, almost stumbling. Her head tilted to one side, and blood covered her chin.

A security guard reached for her arm.

She turned and bit him.

The guard screamed and tried to pull away. The woman clung to him with a strength that looked wrong, her teeth buried in his forearm.

For a moment, everyone stared.

Then the crowd erupted.

People ran in every direction. A tray crashed to the floor. Someone fell. Children began crying.

The woman released the guard and lunged at the nearest shopper.

Aarav felt the blood drain from his face.

He looked at his friends.

No one was laughing anymore.

"Guys," he whispered.

The screaming grew louder.

"That's not a prank."
