# Chapter 5 — The Face Beneath the Hands

Aria stared at the staircase.

The crying continued above them.

Soft.

Slow.

Patient.

Joseph's hand was still wrapped around her arm.

"Don't look upstairs," he whispered.

Aria nodded.

She kept her eyes on him.

But the sound was getting closer.

Sob.

A floorboard creaked above them.

Then another.

Someone was walking down the stairs.

Joseph's fingers tightened around Aria's arm.

"Whatever happens," he whispered, "keep looking at me."

Aria did.

She didn't look away.

She didn't look toward the staircase.

She watched Joseph's face instead.

His eyes were fixed on hers.

The footsteps continued.

One step.

Then another.

The crying became louder.

Aria could hear someone breathing.

Slowly.

Almost peacefully.

Then Joseph whispered,

"She's behind me."

Aria's heart stopped.

She could see Joseph.

She could see the wall behind him.

But she couldn't see the person standing there.

"Don't look," he said.

Aria swallowed.

"How do you know?"

"I can feel her."

The footsteps stopped.

Silence.

Then Joseph began crying.

Not loudly.

Just a single tear rolling down his cheek.

Aria stared at him.

"Joseph?"

He didn't answer.

Another tear.

Then another.

His expression changed.

Fear disappeared from his face.

Something else replaced it.

Recognition.

He whispered,

"She's showing me."

"Showing you what?"

"The first night."

Aria didn't understand.

Then Joseph's eyes moved slightly.

Not toward the staircase.

Toward something beyond Aria.

His lips parted.

"She has no face."

Aria's stomach tightened.

"What?"

Joseph suddenly screamed.

The sound echoed through the house.

Aria flinched.

For one second, her eyes closed.

When she opened them---

Joseph was gone.

His chair was empty.

The room was silent.

Aria stood alone.

"Joseph?"

No answer.

She turned toward the staircase.

The stairs were empty.

Then she noticed something on the floor.

A trail of water.

It began at the bottom of the staircase.

And disappeared into the hallway.

Aria followed it.

Not because she wanted to.

Because she had no idea what else to do.

The trail ended at a closed door.

Joseph's study.

She opened it.

Inside, hundreds of papers covered the walls.

Photographs.

Newspaper clippings.

Names.

Dates.

And one sentence written repeatedly across the walls:

SHE DOES NOT MOVE WHEN YOU WATCH HER.

Aria stepped inside.

Her eyes moved across the photographs.

Forty-three people.

Forty-three missing persons.

All connected to St. Mary's Cemetery.

And every photograph contained the angel.

Aria picked up an old newspaper.

The headline read:

LOCAL GIRL DISAPPEARS FROM CEMETERY

The photograph beneath it showed Joseph's sister.

Aria looked closer.

The girl was standing beside the angel.

Her hands covered her face.

Aria's breathing stopped.

The girl was crying.

But the angel behind her wasn't.

Aria turned the photograph over.

A message had been written on the back.

THE ANGEL WASN'T THERE WHEN WE ARRIVED.

Aria read it again.

Then she understood.

The statue wasn't the beginning.

It was the end.

Something had been happening to people before they disappeared.

The statue came afterward.

A record.

A witness.

A prison.

She searched through more photographs.

Different years.

Different people.

Different faces.

But always the same pattern.

Someone stood beside the angel.

Someone was crying.

And eventually---

they disappeared.

Aria's hands began shaking.

Then she found a photograph she had never expected to see.

Her uncle.

Standing beside the angel.

He was younger.

Maybe twenty years younger.

Beside him stood Joseph.

And between them---

a woman.

Aria recognized her immediately.

Her mother.

Aria dropped the photograph.

"No..."

Her mother had been here.

She had stood beside the angel.

Years before Aria was born.

She picked up the photograph again.

There was writing on the back.

WE THOUGHT WE COULD SAVE HER.

Aria's heart pounded.

Save who?

Her mother?

Her uncle?

The angel?

Then she noticed something in the photograph.

Her mother's hand was holding something.

A small silver button.

Exactly like the one Aria had found in her apartment.

She stepped backward.

A sound came from behind her.

Sob.

Aria froze.

The study door slowly creaked open.

She stared at the doorway.

The hallway was empty.

Then a wet footprint appeared on the wooden floor.

Another appeared beside it.

Then another.

They were coming toward her.

Aria backed away.

The footprints stopped.

Right outside the study.

A woman's voice whispered:

"Aria."

She looked at the doorway.

The voice came again.

"Your mother knew."

Aria's eyes filled with tears.

"Knew what?"

Silence.

Then---

"Why I cry."

The study light flickered.

Once.

Twice.

Then went out.

Aria stood in complete darkness.

A hand touched her shoulder.

She screamed and turned.

Nothing.

Only darkness.

She raised her phone.

The flashlight came on.

The beam fell across the room.

And there she was.

Standing at the far end of the study.

The angel.

Not a statue.

Not stone.

A woman with enormous folded wings.

Her head bowed.

Her hands covering her face.

Tears running between her fingers.

Aria couldn't breathe.

The angel stood perfectly still.

Aria remembered the rule.

Never look away.

So she stared.

The angel didn't move.

Aria took one step backward.

The angel remained still.

Another step.

Still nothing.

Then Aria noticed something.

The angel's fingers were slowly separating.

Just enough to reveal one eye.

An eye filled with tears.

The angel looked directly at her.

And whispered:

"Ask your mother what happened here."
