# The Prince Who Should Not Rise

Years passed beneath the shadow of a stolen crown.

Lord Varen Valen now ruled Aurelion as king, but he was nothing like his brother. Where King Aldric had been brave, kind, and admired… Varen was distant, indifferent, and weak.

The people whispered.

"He does not listen."

"He does not care."

"He is no king."

Yet none dared to speak too loudly.

## The Prince's Path

Within the palace walls, Prince Caelan Valen grew.

Not in luxury.

Not in comfort.

But in steel.

From a young age, he was sent to the training grounds instead of royal halls. While other princes learned politics, Caelan learned war.

Sword in hand.

Dust beneath his feet.

Pain as his teacher.

By the age of fifteen, he had already become a warrior.

And despite everything…

He loved his uncle.

## A Dangerous Strength

As years passed, the people began to notice something.

The prince was different.

Stronger.

Braver.

Kinder.

More like the king they had lost.

"He is Aldric reborn," they whispered.

And that… made Varen uneasy.

## The War

So the king made a decision.

"If he is strong," Varen said coldly, "then let him prove it."

At just fifteen, Prince Caelan was sent to war.

Not to win.

But to die.

Yet fate had other plans.

The boy returned victorious.

Not once.

Not twice.

But every time.

Each battle only made him stronger.

Each victory only made the people love him more.

## A Living Weapon

Killing him was no longer simple.

So Varen chose a different path.

"From this day forward," he declared, "Prince Caelan will serve as my personal guard."

And so it became.

While the king hid behind stone walls…

The prince bled on the battlefield.

The people began to question.

"Why send him to war so young?"

Varen only smiled.

"A future king must learn."

But deep inside, he feared the truth.

## The Prince at Twenty

Time passed.

At twenty years old, Caelan had become everything his father once was.

Tall.

Handsome.

Fearless.

A reflection of King Aldric.

Yet one truth still remained hidden from him—

How his parents had died.

## The Invitation

One day, a royal messenger arrived from afar.

It bore the seal of the Kingdom of Velmora.

King Theron Valeris, ruler of Velmora, had announced that his daughter, Princess Seraphina, would choose her husband from among kings and princes across the lands.

Varen saw opportunity.

"Prepare the finest gifts," he ordered.

Gold.

Silks.

Jewels.

And of course…

He took Caelan with him.

## The Court of Velmora

The grand palace of Velmora was filled with rulers from distant kingdoms.

All had come for one purpose.

To win the hand of Princess Seraphina.

Yet one by one, they failed.

Some were rejected.

Some were never even granted an audience.

From behind flowing curtains, Seraphina watched in silence.

Unmoved.

Unimpressed.

Until…

She saw him.

## A Choice of the Heart

Not the king.

But the prince.

Caelan.

Something in her gaze changed.

"Tell my father," she said softly, "I have chosen."

## The Truth Revealed

When King Theron received her answer, he hesitated.

But he had made a promise.

He approached Varen carefully.

"My daughter has made her choice."

Varen smiled confidently.

"Then let us proceed."

Theron's voice lowered.

"She has chosen… Prince Caelan."

The smile vanished.

## A King's Fury

"I came here as king!" Varen snapped.

"Not to be overshadowed by my own blood!"

The court fell silent.

King Theron raised his hands gently.

"She is young… and wishes to marry someone close to her age."

Varen's eyes burned with anger.

"And what of the prince?" he demanded.

## The Meeting

"Let them speak," Theron said.

Caelan was summoned.

He entered Seraphina's chamber quietly.

For a moment, the world seemed to stop.

She stood before him—radiant, like moonlight.

Her eyes, bright as stars.

"Do you accept this union?" she asked softly.

Caelan looked at her…

And forgot everything else.

"Yes."

## A Binding Decision

The choice was made.

The marriage was agreed.

Varen clenched his fists, but he could not refuse—not before so many witnesses.

"Fine," he said coldly.

"But she will not be queen."

King Theron hesitated… then nodded.

"As long as my daughter is happy."

## A Storm Begins

And so, the prince gained love.

The kingdom gained hope.

But the king…

Gained a new reason to hate.
