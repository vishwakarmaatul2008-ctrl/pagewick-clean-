# Chapter 1 — The Hour Without a Sky

At exactly midnight, every clock in the city stopped.

Not slowed.

Not broke.

Stopped.

The second hand of the clock above the old railway station froze at twelve.

The digital clock inside the twenty-four-hour pharmacy went black.

Even the tiny clock on Mira's phone remained fixed at 12:00 AM.

She noticed it while walking home.

Mira stopped beneath a streetlamp and looked at her screen again.

12:00.

She waited.

Nothing.

She refreshed the screen.

Still 12:00.

A strange uneasiness crawled through her chest.

She looked around.

The city was unusually quiet.

Not peaceful.

Quiet.

There were no cars on the main road. No distant horns. No dogs barking from the narrow streets between buildings.

Even the wind had disappeared.

Mira pulled her jacket closer.

"Okay," she whispered. "That's weird."

She started walking again.

Her apartment was only ten minutes away.

Then she saw the sky.

Mira stopped.

Above the buildings, the stars were gone.

Not hidden by clouds.

Gone.

The entire sky looked like a vast sheet of black glass.

She had seen strange things in the city before. Power cuts. Storms. Broken streetlights.

But never this.

Because there was something else.

A faint silver line stretched across the horizon.

It looked almost like a crack.

Mira stared at it.

The line slowly grew brighter.

Then---

BOOM.

A sound rolled through the city.

Every window around her trembled.

Mira instinctively stepped backward.

For a moment, she thought the city itself had moved.

Then the silver line disappeared.

The stars returned.

The streetlights flickered back to life.

A car suddenly appeared at the end of the road, its headlights cutting through the darkness.

Mira's phone vibrated.

She nearly dropped it.

The screen finally changed.

12:01 AM.

She let out a nervous laugh.

"Just a power problem."

She tried to convince herself.

Then another notification appeared.

No number.

No contact name.

Just a message.

Mira opened it.

There were only five words.

THE BARRIER IS GONE.

Her smile disappeared.

She stared at the screen.

A second message appeared.

DO NOT LEAVE YOUR HOME.

Mira's heart began beating faster.

She looked down the empty street.

Someone was standing beneath the streetlamp fifty metres away.

A woman.

Mira couldn't see her face.

The woman wasn't moving.

Mira took one step backward.

Her phone vibrated again.

A third message appeared.

This time, it contained only one sentence.

IT HAS ALREADY FOUND YOU.

The woman at the end of the street slowly raised her head.

And smiled.

Mira ran.
