# Chapter 8 — Beyond the Barrier

The city went dark one street at a time.

Mira stood alone on the abandoned platform, staring at the darkness spreading toward her.

One street.

Then another.

Then another.

It moved like a wave.

Her phone vibrated.

12:51 AM.

Nine minutes remained.

Mira looked at the tall figure standing at the station entrance.

"Who are you?"

It didn't answer.

It stepped into the light.

Mira's heart stopped.

It had no face.

Only smooth, pale skin where its features should have been.

The figure tilted its head.

Then it spoke.

With her mother's voice.

"Mira, come home."

Mira stepped backward.

"No."

The figure took another step.

"You promised."

Mira looked down at the silver pendant.

The little girl.

The other version of herself.

She finally understood.

The barrier hadn't been created to keep the creatures outside.

It had been created to keep memories from crossing between worlds.

And tonight, she had broken that rule.

The faceless figure reached the platform.

Mira turned and ran toward the station exit.

Behind her came footsteps.

Not one pair.

Hundreds.

She didn't look back.

She reached the old gate and pushed it open.

The city outside was completely dark.

Except for one building.

Her apartment.

A single light burned on the seventh floor.

Mira stopped.

Her phone vibrated.

12:55 AM.

Five minutes.

Another message appeared.

GO HOME.

Mira stared at the message.

Then she realized something.

The messages had never been trying to lead her away.

They had been leading her toward the truth.

She ran.

The streets were empty.

The faceless figure followed behind her.

But it never crossed the station gate.

Mira reached her building.

The entrance doors were wide open.

She entered.

The elevator was waiting.

Its display showed:

8

Mira stared at it.

Then she pressed the button.

The doors opened.

The eighth-floor hallway was gone.

Instead, there was a small room.

A child's bedroom.

Mira stepped inside.

On the wall were dozens of drawings.

A house.

A silver door.

A little girl.

And two women holding hands.

Mira picked up one drawing.

At the bottom, written in childish handwriting, were the words:

MIRA + MUM + ME

Tears filled her eyes.

The little girl hadn't been a monster.

She had been alone.

Mira turned around.

Her mother stood in the doorway.

"You finally remembered."

Mira wiped her eyes.

"Why didn't you tell me?"

"Because if you remembered her, the barrier would know."

"And now?"

Her mother smiled sadly.

"Now it does."

The clock on the wall changed.

12:59 AM.

One minute.

Mira looked at her mother.

"What happens when it becomes one?"

"The barrier resets."

"And me?"

Her mother stepped closer.

"You have a choice."

"What choice?"

"Forget again."

Mira looked at the silver pendant.

Then at the drawings.

She shook her head.

"No."

Her mother smiled.

"I knew you would say that."

The clock reached---

1:00 AM.

Everything went silent.

The room disappeared.

Mira opened her eyes.

She was standing outside her apartment building.

Morning sunlight filled the street.

Cars moved.

People walked.

The city was normal again.

Her phone showed:

7:42 AM.

Mira looked around.

Nobody remembered the night.

Nobody except her.

She reached beneath her shirt.

The silver pendant was still there.

Then her phone vibrated.

Mira froze.

There was one new message.

No number.

No name.

Only four words:

SHE IS STILL HERE.

Mira slowly looked up.

Across the street stood an old woman.

She was holding the hand of a little girl.

The child looked at Mira.

And smiled.

Mira smiled back.

Then the little girl whispered something.

Even from across the street, Mira could hear her.

"I'll see you tonight."

Mira looked at the sky.

For the first time in eleven years, she could see every star.

But among them was one strange silver light.

It wasn't a star.

It was a crack.

And it was getting wider.

The End.
