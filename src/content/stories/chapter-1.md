# Truth or Dare

"Guys, stop drinking already."

Irene looked around the living room with a helpless sigh. Empty bottles and snack packets covered the table while loud music echoed through the house.

"It's only ten o'clock," she said. "We have the whole night ahead of us. Can't we do something more interesting?"

Iris, who had been lying lazily on the couch, immediately sat up.

"I agree. We've had this same party a hundred times. I want to do something exciting."

Jackson wrapped an arm around her shoulders with a teasing smile.

"So... we have to do something exciting because my babe is bored?"

"Oh, shut up," Iris laughed, pushing him away.

Tom couldn't help grinning.

"That's so cheesy."

Magnus stretched his arms confidently.

"I actually agree with Iris. Let's do something fun."

Ace looked around the room.

"Like what?"

Nick adjusted his glasses before speaking.

"Truth or Dare."

Ace sighed.

"We've played that game so many times."

Nick smiled. "Not like this. This time the dares have to be difficult. If someone refuses, they have to host the next house party."

Everyone exchanged excited looks.

"Deal!"

The seven friends sat in a circle while Iris picked up an empty bottle.

"Let's begin."

She spun it.

Round and round it turned before finally stopping...

...right in front of Tom.

A mischievous smile appeared on Iris's face.

"So, Tom..."

"Do you like Irene?"

The room immediately burst into laughter.

Tom scratched the back of his neck awkwardly before smiling.

"...Yeah."

"I like her."

Everyone started teasing him.

Irene simply shook her head.

She already knew.

Tom had flirted with plenty of girls before. Every few months he seemed to have a new crush, and somehow every relationship ended the same way.

He always acted brave around girls...

Until real courage was required.

Iris spun the bottle again.

This time it stopped in front of Magnus.

Nick folded his arms.

"Truth or Dare?"

Magnus laughed proudly.

"Dare."

"I expected that."

Nick pointed toward the dark window.

"You see that abandoned mansion behind the forest?"

The room instantly became quiet.

"I dare you to touch its front gate."

Irene frowned.

"Nick... that's too much."

Ace nodded.

"Yeah. Everyone knows that place has creepy rumors."

Tom swallowed nervously.

"My grandfather always says whoever enters that mansion never comes back."

Magnus laughed loudly.

"That's because nobody has ever gone inside."

Nick smirked.

"So..."

"The brave guy is scared?"

Magnus immediately stood up.

"I'm not scared."

"I won't just touch the gate."

"I'm going inside."

Tom quickly shook his head.

"Magnus, that's a terrible idea. Especially at night."

Magnus ignored him and turned toward Jackson.

"Jackson."

"You brought your car, right?"

Jackson smiled casually.

"Yeah."

"Can you drive us there?"

"Sure. Why not?"

Before Jackson could move...

Iris grabbed his arm.

"No."

Jackson looked at her in surprise.

"What happened?"

"You're not going."

Magnus rolled his eyes.

"Relax. It's just an old abandoned mansion."

"It'll only take a few minutes."

Iris refused to let go.

"I don't care."

"If you go..."

"I'm coming too."

Jackson sighed with a helpless smile.

"Fine."

Ace shrugged.

"Well... now I'm curious too."

Nick grinned.

"I started the challenge."

"So obviously I'm coming."

Now only Irene and Tom remained.

Tom looked toward Irene.

"So..."

"What do we do?"

Irene looked at everyone climbing toward Jackson's car.

She didn't want to go.

Every instinct told her this was a bad idea.

But she also didn't want to stay behind while everyone else went together.

"...Let's go."

---

Jackson started the engine.

The road soon disappeared into the darkness outside the city.

Iris sat in the passenger seat, nervously staring through the window.

The deeper they drove into the forest...

The quieter everyone became.

"I don't like this," Iris whispered.

"The trees... the darkness..."

"It feels wrong."

Jackson smiled reassuringly without taking his eyes off the road.

"Don't worry."

"I'm right here."

"I won't let anything happen to you."

The full moon hung above the forest.

Cold wind shook the trees as Jackson finally stopped the car.

Everyone stepped outside.

Silence.

No birds.

No insects.

Only the sound of the wind.

Then...

They saw it.

The mansion.

It stood at the end of the road like a forgotten giant.

Its walls were cracked.

Its windows reflected the moonlight.

Vines covered almost every wall.

It looked abandoned...

Yet strangely alive.

Tom whispered,

"I really don't think we should be here."

Even Magnus stared for a second before smiling again.

"Looks even cooler than I imagined."

Irene couldn't take her eyes off the mansion.

For some reason...

Her chest suddenly felt heavy.

As if someone inside already knew they had arrived.

Nick broke the silence.

"Let's finish the dare."

"Touch the gate..."

"And go home."

Magnus walked toward the rusted iron gate.

He reached out and placed his hand on it.

"There."

"Dare completed."

Everyone expected him to turn around.

Instead...

He grabbed the gate with both hands.

The rusted metal groaned loudly.

"Creeeeak..."

The gate barely moved.

Magnus laughed.

"It's stuck."

"Jackson."

"Give me a hand."

Jackson walked over.

"No problem."

Together they pushed.

The gate slowly opened.

The sound echoed through the empty forest.

"Jackson!" Iris shouted.

"Come back!"

Nick stepped forward.

"Magnus, stop."

"You already proved you're brave."

Ace nodded.

"Yeah. Let's leave."

"This is getting serious over a stupid game."

Irene looked at Magnus.

"This is enough."

"We shouldn't be trespassing."

But it was already too late.

The gate stood completely open.

Magnus smiled.

"Come on."

"We'll explore for a few minutes."

"We'll record everything."

"Imagine everyone's reaction when they hear we actually entered the Haunted Mansion."

He walked up to the front door.

Everyone expected it to be locked.

Instead...

The old wooden door slowly opened on its own.

Without resistance.

Without a sound.

As though...

It had been waiting for someone.

Magnus smiled.

"Let's go, Jackson."

Jackson nodded.

"Sure, dude."

"No!" Iris shouted before immediately running after him.

Ace sighed.

"We'd better bring those idiots back."

Nick adjusted his glasses and followed.

Tom looked nervously at Irene.

"I have a really bad feeling about this."

"So do I..."

Irene whispered.

Yet...

She stepped inside anyway.

As darkness swallowed the group, one thought echoed through her mind.

Can we all come through this mansion dare.
