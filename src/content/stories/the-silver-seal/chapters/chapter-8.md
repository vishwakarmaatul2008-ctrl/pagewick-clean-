# Chapter 8: Alex's Retaliation

The silence in the central glass atrium was absolute. Every eye was locked on Alex Sterling's broad frame standing tall, his hand still firmly laced through Serena's cold fingers.

Julian's smug expression crumbled instantly from the second-floor balcony. His face flushed bright red as he gripped the brass railing, his knuckles turning white. "You're bluffing, Sterling! You don't have access to those files!"

"Try me, Julian," Alex said, his voice flat, dangerously calm, and completely unyielding. "You think your father's hedge fund is invisible? I had my family's legal team audit your domestic transfers the moment you opened your mouth yesterday. One click, and the board isn't investigating a scholarship—they're handing your family a federal summons."

Max stepped forward, casually pulling a sleek black tablet from his jacket and tossing it onto the dean assistant's desk. "And here are the raw server logs," Max added, his usual lazy grin replaced by a sharp, lethal edge. "It traces the exact IP address that uploaded those forged bank statements straight to Julian's private dorm network twenty minutes ago."

The dean's assistant looked down at the screen, her eyes widening in realization. She picked up the tablet, shot a cold look up at Julian on the balcony, and tapped her phone.

"Mr. Vance," she said into the receiver, her voice loud enough for the atrium to hear. "The Disciplinary Board expects you in office 102 immediately. Do not pass go."

Julian backed away from the railing in sheer panic, turning on his heel and disappearing down the hallway as the crowd exploded into hushed, shocked chatter.

Alex didn't wait to watch him leave. Without dropping Serena's hand, he turned and led her straight through the parted crowd, past the whispering students, and out into the quiet courtyard toward his car. Chloe and Max followed close behind, shielding them from any lingering eyes.

When they reached the quiet shade of the VIP parking bay, Alex finally stopped and turned to face her. His grip softened, but he didn't let go of her hand.

"Are you okay?" he asked, his dark eyes searching her face with an intensity that made her breath catch.

Serena looked up at him, her chest heaving as the adrenaline slowly drained from her system. "You... you threatened his entire family's fund for me? Alex, you didn't have to put your name on the line like that."

"I told you last night," Alex whispered, stepping closer until his broad chest brushed against her shoulders, his voice dropping into a low, possessive baritone that made her heart slam against her ribs. "Nobody touches what is mine. I meant every word."

Serena's breath hitched as his hand slid up from her fingers to cradle the back of her neck, pulling her gently toward him right there in the open courtyard, completely uncaring of who might be watching.
