# Chapter 6: The Secret Kiss

The heat in the locked office was thick enough to suffocate. Alex's hand remained anchored at the nape of Serena's neck, his fingers tangled firmly in her soft blonde hair, holding her still as his thumb gently stroked the line of her jaw.

Serena's heart was hammering against her ribs like a trapped bird. Her hands, still resting on his broad chest, could feel the rapid, heavy thud of his own heart. The cold, unreadable elite who ruled St. Jude's with absolute authority was breathing hard, his composure shattered by her mere proximity.

"Alex..." she gasped, her voice trembling as his lips hovered a fraction of an inch from hers. "We can't..."

"Why?" Alex whispered against her skin, his deep baritone sending an electric shiver straight down her spine. "Give me one real reason, Serena. Tell me you don't feel this."

"Because you're Alex Sterling," she breathed, her hazel eyes searching his dark, burning gaze. "And I'm just a scholarship student trying to survive here. If anyone sees us—"

"Let them look," Alex growled softly.

Before she could form another thought, he closed the remaining distance.

His lips captured hers in a deep, possessive kiss that wiped every trace of logic from her mind. Serena gasped softly against his mouth, and Alex used the opportunity to deepen the kiss, pulling her waist flush against his broad chest until there was no space left between them.

An electric shock surged through Serena's veins. All the built-up tension, the stolen glances in crowded lecture halls, the forced proximity in his car—it all exploded in a single, breathless moment. Her fingers curled into the smooth fabric of his shirt, pulling him closer as she kissed him back, completely surrendering to the overwhelming fire consuming them both.

Alex let out a low, rough groan against her lips. His grip on her waist tightened, lifting her slightly off her feet against the edge of the heavy mahogany desk. His mouth moved over hers with an unhurried, intoxicating demand, tasting her, claiming her, refusing to let her go.

When he finally pulled back just an inch, both of them were completely out of breath. Their foreheads rested together in the dim light of the desk lamp, their warm breath mingling in the quiet air.

Alex's dark eyes opened, staring down at her flushed face and swollen lips with a look of pure, unadulterated victory.

"You're not just surviving here anymore, Serena," Alex murmured, his thumb brushing over her lower lip, tracing the wet sheen left behind by his kiss. "You're mine. And everyone on this campus is going to learn that real fast."

Serena looked up at him, her chest heaving, realizing with a sudden, terrifying thrill that there was no going back. The line had been crossed—and she didn't want to turn back.
