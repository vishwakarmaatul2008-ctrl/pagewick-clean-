# Chapter 7: The Rumor Mill

The crisp morning air inside the central glass atrium was suffocatingly quiet. Serena walked toward the main staircase, her hands tightened around the straps of her tote bag. She had barely slept. Every time she closed her eyes, she felt the warm press of Alex's lips against hers, the rough texture of his hands, and the possessive weight of his words echoing in the dark loft.

"Serena, wait," Chloe's voice called out sharply from behind.

Serena turned to see Chloe rushing toward her, her rich brunette hair swinging over her shoulders. Chloe's face was uncharacteristically pale, her phone gripped tightly in her hand.

"What's wrong?" Serena asked, her stomach instantly dropping.

Instead of answering, Chloe flipped her phone around and shoved the screen in front of Serena's face. It was the official St. Jude's student forum—a private, high-traffic board where the campus elite traded gossip.

At the very top of the feed, pinned in bold red text, was a thread posted less than an hour ago:

*[EXCLUSIVE] SCHOLARSHIP FRAUD: Is Serena Moore faking her financial records to stay at St. Jude's? Documents leaked online.*

Serena's breath hitched. She scrolled rapidly through the post. Attached were edited bank statements and a forged letter claiming her financial aid application contained fraudulent declarations regarding her part-time job income.

"This is completely fake," Serena whispered, her voice shaking as the color drained from her face. "I submitted every official tax document directly to the dean's office. I don't—who would do this?"

"Julian," Chloe said coldly, her eyes flashing with anger. "Or someone in his circle. He couldn't handle Alex putting him in his place yesterday, so he's trying to trigger an automatic board review before you can prove it's fake."

As if on cue, subtle whispers rippled through the surrounding atrium. Students gathered near the grand pillars were looking over, pointing at their screens, and throwing sharp, judgment-filled glances in Serena's direction.

Serena felt her throat lock up. The scholarship was her entire life—her ticket to a future outside of endless double shifts and crushing debt. If the board suspended her pending an investigation, she would lose her housing by the end of the week.

"Serena Moore?"

A sharp, middle-aged woman in a grey tailored suit stepped into the atrium, holding an iPad. It was Dean Vance's executive assistant. "The Disciplinary Board requires your immediate presence in Building A. Please come with me."

Panic flared in Serena's chest. "I didn't forge anything—"

"Save it for the board, Moore," a sneering voice called out from the second-floor balcony.

Julian Vance leaned over the brass railing, a smug, triumphant grin plastered across his face as he swirled a cup of iced coffee. "Rules are rules. Charity cases shouldn't cheat the system."

Serena took a step back, her hands trembling.

Before the dean's assistant could reach out to escort her, heavy, rapid footsteps echoed across the marble floor.

A tall, broad frame cut cleanly through the crowd, stepping directly into Serena's path and blocking her from the assistant.

Alex Sterling had arrived. His dark blazer was open, his tie slightly loosened, and his carved face was set in a expression of pure, unbridled fury. Right beside him was Max, whose usual charismatic smirk had completely vanished, replaced by a cold, deadly serious gaze fixed straight on Julian.

Alex didn't look at the whispering crowd. His dark, burning eyes locked directly onto Serena's pale face, taking in her trembling hands and the unshed tears in her eyes.

"Alex," Serena whispered, her voice breaking. "The board—"

"Shut up, Serena," Alex said softly, though his voice carried an absolute, unwavering strength that instantly grounded her. He reached out, taking her cold, trembling hand in his large, warm palm, lacing his long fingers firmly through hers right in front of the entire atrium.

Gasps rippled through the onlookers. The elite golden boy of St. Jude's was holding hands with the scholarship girl in broad daylight.

Alex turned around slowly, pulling Serena slightly behind his broad shoulder, his gaze locking onto the dean's assistant and then rising up to Julian on the balcony.

"Tell the board to cancel the hearing," Alex said, his voice quiet, cold, and reverberating off the glass walls like thunder. "Because if they open that file, I release the audit reports on every private account connected to the Vance family by noon."
