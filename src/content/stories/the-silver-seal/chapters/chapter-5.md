# Chapter 5: Midnight Rules

The executive wing was silent by 9:00 PM. High above the courtyard, the loft office was lit only by the warm ambient glow of a single desk lamp and the sprawling city lights outside the floor-to-ceiling windows.

Serena stood by the dark mahogany desk, organizing the finalized event schedules into neat leather binders. Chloe had left an hour ago after a sharp, verbal sparring match with Max over budget files, leaving Serena alone in the quiet space.

Or so she thought.

The soft heavy click of the solid oak door locking echoed through the room. Serena turned quickly, her hand pausing on the desk.

Alex stood at the entrance, holding two crystal glasses filled with iced water. He had removed his suit jacket and unbuttoned the top three buttons of his black dress shirt, rolling the fitted sleeves up to his elbows. The relaxed, private posture only highlighted his broad frame and raw physical presence.

"You're still here," Alex said softly, his voice echoing in the still room.

"I wanted to finish these files so you wouldn't have an excuse to call me early tomorrow," Serena replied, her hazel eyes tracking him as he walked slowly toward her.

Alex placed the glasses on the edge of the desk, stopping just inches from where she stood. "You don't need an excuse to be here, Serena."

The temperature in the room instantly spiked. Serena felt her pulse jump into her throat as Alex reached out, his large hand taking the binder straight from her fingers and tossing it casually onto the desk behind her.

"What are you doing?" she whispered, her breath catching.

"Establishing the new rules," Alex murmured, stepping directly into her personal space until the back of her legs pressed against the smooth edge of the mahogany desk. He leaned in, placing both hands on the wood on either side of her hips, effectively trapping her between his broad body and the desk.

"Alex..." Serena's voice was barely audible.

"Rule number one," Alex said, his low baritone dropping an octave, vibrating right against her skin. "When we are in this room, you don't look at the floor. You look at me."

Serena lifted her gaze, her hazel eyes locking into his dark, burning intensity. The cold, calculating mask he wore for the rest of St. Jude's was completely gone, replaced by a raw, possessive hunger that made her knees weak.

"Rule number two," Alex continued, his gaze dipping slowly down to her lips before rising back to her eyes. "You don't thank me for protecting you from idiots like Julian. It's not an option. Nobody touches what is mine."

Serena gasped softly, the word echoing in her mind. "Mine?"

"You heard me," Alex whispered. He raised one hand, his warm, rough knuckles slowly tracing the sensitive curve of her neck, sliding upward until his thumb rested right against the pulse point hammering rapidly at her throat. "You think this assistant deal was about a spilled coffee or a paint job?"

"Wasn't it?" Serena asked, her hands rising instinctively to rest against his chest, feeling the solid, rapid beat of his heart beneath the thin fabric of his shirt.

"It was the only way I could keep you close without you running away," Alex admitted, his dark eyes softening with a dangerous honesty.

He leaned down, his face closing the final distance between them. Serena held her breath, her eyes fluttering shut as his warm lips brushed lightly against the corner of her mouth—a slow, agonizingly deliberate touch that sent an electric surge straight through her core.

He didn't take the kiss completely. He teased the edge of her lips, his warm breath fanning across her face, leaving her completely breathless and begging for more.

"Midnight rules, Serena," Alex whispered against her skin, his hand tightening softly in her blonde hair, pulling her head back just enough to force her to look at him. "Starting tonight, everything changes."
