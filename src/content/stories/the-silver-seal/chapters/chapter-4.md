# Chapter 4: Broken Shields

The heavy silence inside the car was broken only by the steady rhythm of rain against the glass. Alex's hand remained tangled in Serena's damp blonde hair, his thumb tracing the soft curve of her jawline while his dark eyes stayed fixed on her parted lips.

Serena couldn't move. The air between them felt thick, charged with an undeniable friction that had nothing to do with assistant duties or damaged property.

"Alex..." Her voice was a fragile whisper, her chest rising and falling rapidly against the tight space.

"Tell me to stop," Alex said, his deep voice dropping into an unhurried, commanding murmur. His face was barely two inches from hers, his broad shoulder completely blocking out the rest of the world. "Tell me you want me to back away, Serena. Say it once, and I'll drop you off at your dorm and never touch you again."

Serena's heart slammed against her ribs. Every logical part of her brain—the part that valued her scholarship, her clean record, and her peaceful academic life—demanded that she tell him to back off. But looking into his dark, intense eyes, the words caught in her throat. She didn't move. She didn't pull away.

A slow, dangerous smirk touched the corner of Alex's lips. "That's what I thought."

He didn't force a kiss. Instead, he leaned in closer, his lips brushing softly against the sensitive skin right beneath her ear, sending a sudden, electric shiver straight down her spine. Serena gasped, her hands instinctively coming up to rest against his broad chest, feeling the solid, radiating heat through his shirt.

Before the moment could spiral completely out of control, Alex pulled back slowly. His fingers slid out of her hair, though his gaze remained locked on hers, dark and thoroughly unreadable. Without another word, he shifted into drive, pulled out of the deserted VIP lot, and cut through the stormy night toward the campus housing district.

The next morning, the storm had cleared, leaving the university courtyard bathed in bright, crisp sunlight. Serena walked alongside Chloe toward the central lecture hall, trying her best to ignore the subtle lingering warmth on her neck from the previous night.

"You're quiet," Chloe noted, glancing at her with sharp, intelligent brown eyes. "What happened after I left the lounge yesterday? Did Sterling give you a hard time about the notes?"

"No," Serena said quickly, fixing the strap of her bag. "Just... standard filing. Everything is under control."

Chloe raised an eyebrow, clearly unconvinced, but before she could pry further, a loud voice echoed across the crowded marble hallway.

"Well, if it isn't the scholarship charity case."

Serena stopped cold. Standing near the grand staircase was Julian Vance—Max's distant cousin and one of the most notoriously arrogant seniors on campus—surrounded by a small circle of wealthy upperclassmen.

Julian stepped forward, holding a printed sheet of paper with a condescending smirk. "Word on campus is you're already desperate for money, Moore. Working late shifts at some cheap joint down the street just to stay afloat? St. Jude's really lowered its standards this year."

A few surrounding students paused, turning their heads to watch the scene unfold. Serena felt the color drain from her face, her hands clenching into tight fists at her sides.

"Watch your mouth, Julian," Chloe snapped instantly, stepping in front of Serena with her eyes flashing fire. "You don't talk to her like that."

"Relax, Bennett," Julian sneered, taking another step closer to Serena. "I'm just offering a solution. If you need cash that badly, Moore, you don't have to scrub tables. I could hire you to—"

"You could hire who?"

The low, icy voice cut through the hallway like a blade.

The surrounding crowd instantly went quiet as Alex Sterling walked into the corridor, flanked by Max. Alex was dressed in a tailored black suit jacket over a crisp white shirt, his imposing 6'2" frame instantly dominating the space. His face was a mask of cold absolute authority, but his eyes were burning with a dark, lethal intensity as they locked onto Julian.

Julian's smirk faltered slightly, but he tried to maintain his ground. "Sterling. Just letting the new girl know how things work around here—"

Alex closed the distance in three deliberate steps. He didn't raise his voice, but as he stepped directly between Julian and Serena, his broad shoulders completely shielded her from view.

"She works for me," Alex said, his tone flat, calm, and terrifyingly absolute. "Which means if you have something to say to her, you say it to me first."

Julian swallowed hard, taking an involuntary half-step back under the weight of Alex's glare. "It was just a joke, Alex."

"It's not a joke," Max chimed in from the side, a cold smile replacing his usual charismatic grin as he stepped up beside Chloe. "And if we hear your voice anywhere near them again, Julian, we'll have a very different conversation about your family's seat on the athletic board."

Julian held up his hands in defensive silence, turning on his heel and walking away quickly with his group following close behind.

The surrounding students quickly dispersed, leaving the four of them standing in the center of the grand hallway. Serena looked up at the back of Alex's dark jacket, her pulse hammering in her ears.

Alex turned around slowly. His cold mask remained firmly in place, but as his eyes swept over Serena's face, checking for any sign of distress, his voice softened by a fraction.

"Are you alright?" he asked quietly.

Serena looked into his dark eyes, realizing with a sudden, terrifying clarity that the wall she had built to keep him at a distance was officially crumbling.

"I'm fine," she whispered. "Thank you."

Alex stared at her for a long second before giving a single, subtle nod. "My office. 4:00 PM. Don't be late."
