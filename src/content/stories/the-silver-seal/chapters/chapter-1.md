# Chapter 1: The Silver Seal

The heavy glass doors of the grand library swung open, letting in the humid afternoon heat as Serena Moore stepped out onto the granite courtyard. She adjusted the strap of her tote bag, balancing a cardboard tray of fresh, piping-hot dark coffees she had just picked up. Her thumb rested briefly over the raised silver lettering on her campus ID: Full Academic Scholar. With her long, radiant blonde hair framing a face naturally framed by large, strikingly intelligent hazel eyes, Serena effortlessly drew subtle glances from passing students, though her focus remained strictly grounded on her work ethic.

She had survived seven days at St. Jude's University. Seven days of navigating marble archways, high-tech lecture halls, and avoiding the ultra-wealthy elite who treated the campus like their private playground.

"Watch out!" Chloe Bennett called out, her heels clicking rapidly on the smooth stone as she ran up behind her. Chloe was breathtaking in a completely different way—a gorgeous brunette with rich, glossy brown hair falling over her shoulders, dressed in a high-end designer sweater that matched her impeccably groomed, vibrant confidence.

Serena turned her head quickly, but her shoe caught the edge of an uneven cobblestone. Her balance broke. She stumbled forward with a quiet gasp, her arms flying out to break her fall. The tray flipped completely out of her hands, sending two full cups of dark, scalding coffee pouring directly over the sleek, immaculate silver hood of a custom sports car parked in the VIP executive slot.

The dark liquid splattered across the polished metal, running down the headlights and pooling onto the pristine front grill.

"Oh my god," Serena gasped, her face draining of color as she scrambled to her feet. "No, no, no..."

Before she could grab a napkin from her bag, heavy footsteps echoed across the courtyard steps.

Walking toward the VIP bay were two tall, striking figures who moved with absolute authority. On the left was Max Vance—standing at 6'1" with a lean-muscular, athletic posture and a confident, magnetic smile. He was casually spinning a set of car keys on his finger, his sharp features relaxed until his eyes landed on his best friend's car.

Beside him walked Alex Sterling.

Standing at an imposing 6'2", Alex commanded the space around him with a cold, completely unreadable aura. Beneath his fitted dark polo, his build was intensely athletic—broad shoulders, a sculpted chest, and thick biceps. His dark eyes were cold, observant, and sharp as flint. His carved jawline hardened as his gaze locked onto the dark stain dripping off his silver hood.

"Tell me my eyes are playing tricks on me, Sterling," Max murmured, stopping dead in his tracks, his charismatic charm briefly replaced by genuine disbelief. "Did someone just paint your hood with espresso?"

Alex didn't answer. His cold gaze slowly shifted off the car and landed directly on the two girls standing beside it. The temperature around them seemed to drop by ten degrees as he stepped closer, his heavy frame imposing and completely unbothered by the growing crowd around the steps.

"What did you do?" Alex's voice was low, smooth, and dangerously quiet.

Serena swallowed hard, stepping slightly in front of Chloe and holding her hands up. "It was a total accident. I tripped on the stone—I didn't mean to spill anything on your car. I'm so sorry."

Alex took another step forward, his dark, piercing eyes cutting straight through her. "Sorry doesn't fix a custom paint job."

"Look, I can make it up to you," Serena said quickly, her mind racing to find a practical solution. "I work shifts at the local pizza shop down the street to cover my living expenses. I can bring you guys free pizza whenever you want—fresh, custom orders, on me."

Max let out a short, amused laugh, resting a hand on his hip while eyeing her with mild skepticism. "Pizza? You think free pizza covers damage on a high-end sports car?"

"We don't want your pizza," Alex said flatly, his tone cold as ice.

Before Serena could speak, Chloe stepped forward, tossing her rich brunette hair over her shoulder. Her eyes narrowed as she reached into her luxury handbag, pulling out a sleek leather holder and sliding out two gold metal credit cards with total ease.

"Okay, enough with the dramatic staring," Chloe interrupted, stepping right alongside Serena. "Name your price. Tell me the exact amount for the detail work and repaint, and I'll pay it right now. Credit, debit, transfer—whatever you want."

Max's eyebrows raised in mild amusement, while Alex's expression didn't shift by a millimeter. He glanced briefly at the gold cards, then back at Chloe, before his dark eyes settled squarely onto Serena's face.

"We don't need your money," Alex said calmly, leaning back against the side of his sports car and crossing his broad arms over his chest, his thick biceps flexing against his dark sleeves as he studied both of them. "You're the two new scholarship students from the morning lecture hall."

Serena's heart sank. "What does that have to do with anything?"

"Simple," Alex said, his dark eyes locking onto Serena with unwavering intensity. "You can't afford the actual legal bill for this, and we don't care about your friend's credit cards. So here is the deal: you two become our personal assistants for the rest of the semester."

"What?" Chloe snapped, her jaw dropping. "Are you insane? Absolutely not!"

"You say no," Alex continued, his voice utterly calm yet carrying a lethal edge, "and by tomorrow morning, everyone on this campus hears about the scholarship girl who vandalized private property on her first week. Rumors travel fast here. Let's see how long your academic standing lasts when the board gets an anonymous file."

Serena's breath hitched. Her entire future at St. Jude's depended on keeping her head down, maintaining her 3.9 GPA, and keeping her reputation completely clean.

She looked up at Alex—his cold, carved face waiting for her answer without a single trace of mercy.
