# Chapter 2: The First Assignment

The morning air inside St. Jude's private executive wing was heavy with the rich aroma of roasted espresso and cold marble. Serena held her breath as she pushed open the polished oak door of the student lounge—a secluded, glass-walled loft reserved strictly for the university's elite.

Chloe walked beside her, her heels clicking softly against the hardwood floor. "I still can't believe we're doing this," Chloe whispered under her breath, though her sharp eyes swept the room with calm defiance. "One word from him about a board report, and we're playing personal staff."

"It's one semester," Serena murmured, adjusting the strap of her leather tote. "We keep our heads down, do whatever stupid tasks they assign, and keep my scholarship clean."

"Ah, the new administrative team has arrived," a smooth, charismatic voice called out.

Max Vance leaned against the mahogany kitchen island, dressed in a tailored charcoal blazer with the top two buttons of his white shirt undone. He held a crystal tumbler of iced water, a lazy, amused grin playing on his lips as his eyes drifted over Chloe.

"You're five minutes late," Max added, taking a slow sip.

"We had lecture, Vance," Chloe snapped, stepping forward and crossing her arms. "We aren't here to entertain you."

"You're here because you owe a custom paint job," a low, dark voice chimed from the back of the loft.

Serena's heart skipped a beat.

Alex Sterling stepped out from the shadow of his private office corner. He had shed his jacket, wearing only a fitted black shirt with the sleeves rolled up to his elbows, revealing sculpted forearms veins tracing down to his hands. Standing at 6'2", his broad frame instantly made the spacious loft feel suffocatingly small.

His dark, cold eyes swept over Serena, lingering on the subtle rise and fall of her chest before settling on her hazel eyes.

"Your schedules," Alex said, tossing two thick black leather folders onto the glass coffee table between them. "Every morning before 8:00 AM, my coffee is on my desk. Black, no sugar, 185 degrees. Serena handles my itinerary and lecture notes. Chloe handles Max's."

"I don't take orders from someone whose main personality trait is a scowl," Chloe said smoothly, stepping right up to the mahogany island, inches from Max.

Max's grin widened. He leaned down slightly, his 6'1" frame towering over her, his voice dropping into a smooth, teasing murmur. "Careful, Bennett. You talk big for someone whose gold cards couldn't buy her out of this room."

Chloe's breath caught, her rich brown eyes flashing with irritation as Max's gaze dipped deliberately to her lips before flashing a lazy smile.

Meanwhile, Alex hadn't moved a muscle. He walked slowly toward Serena, his heavy, deliberate footsteps echoing in the quiet room. Serena instinctively took a half-step back, but her spine bumped into the soft leather edge of the central sofa.

Alex didn't stop until he was standing directly in front of her. The heat radiating off his broad body was overwhelming, carrying the crisp scent of cedarwood, rain, and expensive cologne.

He leaned down, placing one heavy hand on the leather sofa cushion right beside her hip, trapping her in his space.

"You look nervous, Moore," Alex quieted his voice, the deep baritone sending an electric shiver straight down her spine.

Serena forced herself to hold his gaze, refusing to look away even as her pulse hammered in her throat. "I'm not nervous. I'm just waiting for my first real assignment so I can get out of here."

Alex's gaze dropped to her lips. The cold, unreadable mask he usually wore cracked for a split second, replaced by a dark, intense flicker of visual hunger that made Serena's knees go weak. He reached out, his long fingers brushing against a loose strand of her blonde hair, gently tucking it behind her ear. His knuckles lightly grazed the sensitive skin of her jawline.

Serena gasped softly at the sudden, unexpected warmth of his touch.

"Your first assignment," Alex whispered, his face inches from hers, his dark eyes locking onto her hazel ones with suffocating intensity, "is learning how to stand still when I'm in the room."

He pulled back slowly, taking his hand off the sofa and leaving Serena breathless, her skin tingling where his fingers had touched her.

"Desk notes by tomorrow morning, Serena," Alex said, turning his back as if nothing had happened, though the air in the room remained charged with raw, undeniable tension. "Don't be late."
