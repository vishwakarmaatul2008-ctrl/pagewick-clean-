# Chapter 3: The Car Scene

The autumn rain poured over St. Jude's campus in heavy, relentless sheets, blurring the amber glow of the courtyard streetlamps. It was past 8:00 PM. The library had closed, leaving the parking lot completely deserted—except for the sleek, low profile of the silver sports car sitting under the dim light of the VIP awning.

Serena pulled her jacket tightly around herself, holding her canvas tote bag over her head as she rushed down the granite steps. Her shoes clicked against the wet pavement, water splashing over her ankles.

Before she could run toward the main gates, a dark window rolled down, and a low, familiar horn sounded twice.

"Get in," a deep voice cut through the sound of the falling rain.

Serena stopped, her hazel eyes locking onto Alex Sterling sitting in the driver's seat. He had his dark sleeves rolled up, one wrist resting casually on top of the leather steering wheel. Through the rain-streaked glass, his broad shoulders and sharp, carved profile looked imposing—and completely unavoidable.

"I'm taking the bus," Serena called back over the roar of the rain.

"The last bus left ten minutes ago," Alex said calmly, unlocking the passenger door with a quiet click. "Get in, Serena. I'm not going to ask twice."

Realizing she had zero choice, Serena hurried around the hood, pulled the door open, and slipped into the plush, dark leather interior.

The moment the heavy door shut, the noise of the storm was instantly cut off, replaced by the soft hum of the high-end climate control. The cabin was warm, heavy with the scent of rain, rich leather, and Alex's signature cedarwood cologne.

Serena brushed wet strands of blonde hair off her face, her pulse quickening as she realized how impossibly small the enclosed space felt. Alex didn't pull out of the lot immediately. He shifted in his seat, turning his body toward her, leaning one broad arm over the center console.

His dark, cold eyes slowly traced the damp fabric of her blouse clinging softly to her collarbone, before rising back up to meet her gaze.

"You stayed late finishing the economics filing," Alex said, his voice a low, magnetic baritone in the quiet car.

"I promised I'd handle your notes," Serena replied, forcing her voice to stay steady despite the intense heat radiating off his body. "I keep my word."

Alex stared at her for a long, silent moment. The cold, unreadable mask he wore around campus seemed to dissolve in the dim, warm light of the dashboard. He reached out, his long fingers wrapping around the seatbelt strap near her shoulder.

Instead of pulling it across, he paused. His knuckles lightly grazed the warm skin of her neck.

Serena gasped, her breath catching in her throat as a sharp, undeniable electric jolt shot straight through her spine. Her hazel eyes widened, trapped by the sheer intensity of his gaze.

"You're freezing," Alex murmured, his thumb moving slightly to stroke the edge of her jawline. His touch was firm, possessive, and impossibly warm.

"Alex..." she whispered, her voice barely audible over the hum of the engine.

"What, Serena?" he asked softly, leaning closer.

His broad frame towered over her in the passenger seat, crowding her space until she was completely enveloped by his presence. His dark eyes dipped down to her lips, holding there for three agonizing seconds as he tilted his head. His warm breath fanned across her cheek, inches away from a kiss.

Serena's heart hammered against her ribs like a trapped bird. Every instinct told her to pull back, but her body was frozen, completely mesmerized by the dark, simmering tension crackling between them.

Alex's hand shifted to the back of her neck, his fingers tangling softly into her damp blonde hair, pulling her a fraction of an inch closer.

"You think this is just about a spilled coffee, don't you?" Alex whispered, his voice dangerously low against her skin.

Before she could answer, his thumb pressed gently against her lower lip, parting it slightly as his eyes burned into hers.
