# Chapter 10: Epilogue & Resolution

Three months later, the early winter chill settled over St. Jude's University, frosting the edges of the marble archways and turning the morning courtyard into a sea of wool coats and warm coffees.

The central lawn was bustling with students heading toward final exams, but as Serena walked down the granite steps, the atmosphere was entirely different from her first week on campus. There were no hushed sneers, no cruel whispers, and no arrogant glares. The campus elite stepped aside respectfully as she passed, nodding politely.

Serena wore a sleek tailored coat, her radiant blonde hair pulled back neatly, her hazel eyes bright with confidence. Beside her, Chloe walked with her head held high, holding two warm cups of dark espresso.

"I still think we should have made them buy the pizza shop," Chloe said with a sly smile, taking a sip from her cup.

Serena laughed softly. "I think the official public apology from Julian's legal team was enough."

"Fair," Chloe admitted, her eyes darting toward the edge of the courtyard where a familiar custom silver sports car was parked—no longer in the VIP slot, but in the reserved student board space.

Leaning against the hood were Max and Alex.

Max looked effortlessly sharp in a tailored dark coat, his signature charismatic smirk firmly in place as he caught Chloe's eye. He held up two VIP tickets to an exclusive weekend gala in the city, raising his eyebrows in silent invitation. Chloe rolled her eyes, but a genuine smile tugged at her lips as she walked toward him.

Then Serena's eyes shifted to Alex.

Standing at 6'2", broad-shouldered and imposing, he looked as commanding as ever in a fitted dark turtleneck and black coat. But the moment his dark eyes caught Serena stepping down the courtyard stairs, the icy, unreadable mask he wore for the rest of the world completely melted away. A soft, quiet warmth filled his gaze—a look reserved exclusively for her.

Alex pushed off the car and walked straight toward her, closing the distance between them with his long, deliberate stride.

"You're late, Moore," Alex murmured softly, stopping right in front of her. The cold air carried his signature scent of cedarwood, rain, and subtle cologne.

"I had to hand in my honors research proposal," Serena replied, her lips curving into a soft smirk. "Some of us actually have to maintain that 3.9 GPA."

"You don't have to prove anything to anyone on this campus," Alex said softly. He reached out, his large, warm hand taking hers and pulling her into his broad frame. His long fingers laced through hers, entirely unbothered by the surrounding students watching them.

"I know," Serena whispered, looking up into his dark eyes. "I just like keeping my standards higher than yours."

Alex let out a low, rare laugh—a sound that sent a familiar electric surge straight through Serena's heart. He leaned down, his face closing the distance until his warm lips brushed softly, possessively against hers right in the middle of the crowded courtyard.

The scholarship girl who stepped onto St. Jude's campus trying to remain invisible was gone. In her place stood someone who had conquered her future, earned her place, and captured the heart of the university's coldest elite.

Alex pulled back just a fraction, his thumb tracing the soft line of her jawline as he looked down at her with unfiltered devotion.

"Let's go home, Serena," he whispered against her lips.

Serena smiled, squeezing his hand tightly. "Let's go."

**THE END**
