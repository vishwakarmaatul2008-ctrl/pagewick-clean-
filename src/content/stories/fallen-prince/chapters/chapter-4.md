# The Shadow Behind the Crown

The palace had fallen quiet after the storm of the court.

Inside their chamber, the weight of unspoken tension lingered in the air.

Prince Caelan stood near the window, his jaw tight, his thoughts restless.

The king's words still echoed in his mind—mocking, humiliating, deliberate.

Behind him, Seraphina watched silently.

"Caelan…" she called softly.

He turned.

Her eyes were gentle, but they saw more than he realized.

"Come here."

He hesitated for a moment… then walked toward her.

Seraphina sat upon the bed and gently guided him down, resting his head in her lap. Her fingers moved slowly through his hair—soft, comforting, steady.

"You're thinking too much," she murmured.

He let out a quiet breath. "What happened in the court… it wasn't right."

"It wasn't your fault," she said firmly.

He looked up at her.

"I don't care what they say," she continued, her voice calm yet certain.

"As long as you are with me… nothing else matters."

For a moment, silence filled the room.

Then she asked, softly—

"What about your parents?"

The question lingered.

Caelan's expression changed.

"They died… when I was two," he said quietly.

"I don't remember them."

Seraphina's hand slowed.

"My uncle raised me after that," he continued. "But… he was never really there."

His gaze drifted away.

"I grew up in training grounds. With soldiers. With weapons. My life was war."

A faint smile appeared—but it carried no warmth.

"I learned how to fight before I learned how to live."

Seraphina felt her chest tighten.

"The people of the kingdom loved me," he said. "But… I never felt what a family truly was."

Then his eyes softened as he looked at her.

"Until I met you."

Her breath stilled.

"For the first time…" he whispered, "I feel seen. I feel… loved."

His hand gently reached for hers.

"And I will protect that. No matter what it takes."

Even… if it meant standing against his own blood.

Seraphina smiled softly, continuing to run her fingers through his hair.

But behind her calm expression—

Her thoughts were racing.

Because unlike Caelan…

She was not blind.

She had seen the king's eyes.

Felt the tension in the court.

Heard the hidden cruelty behind his words.

This was not anger.

This was something deeper.

Something dangerous.

## The Silent Decision

From that very night…

Seraphina made a choice.

Without Caelan knowing, she sent a secret messenger beyond the palace walls.

"Find everything," she had ordered quietly.

"About the king… and the past."

And from that day onward—

Caelan spent most of his time by her side.

They walked through the kingdom together.

Met the people.

Shared laughter that the palace walls had long forgotten.

The people adored them.

Their prince.

Their princess.

Their hope.

But within the shadows—

King Varen watched.

And he waited.

## The War That Was Not a War

One morning, the royal court was summoned.

"We have received word," Varen announced, his voice echoing coldly, "that the neighboring kingdom prepares for war."

The hall stirred.

And then—

"Prince Caelan will lead the battle."

Caelan stepped forward without hesitation.

"I will not fail the kingdom."

Varen smiled faintly.

"You will take a small unit," he added.

A murmur spread.

Too small.

Too dangerous.

Too deliberate.

But Caelan did not question it.

He never did.

## A Farewell Filled with Fear

Before leaving, he went to Seraphina.

She stood waiting for him.

Something was wrong.

"I have a bad feeling about this," she whispered, her voice trembling.

"Please… don't go."

Caelan smiled gently.

"You don't need to worry."

She stepped closer, holding onto him tightly.

"I trust you," she said.

"But I don't trust him."

The words lingered.

Caelan's expression hardened slightly.

"You're overthinking."

Tears filled her eyes.

He cupped her face gently.

"Don't cry," he said softly.

"You'll only make me weak."

She took a breath… forcing herself to stop.

"Then I won't be your weakness," she whispered.

"I'll be your strength."

But even as she said it—

Her heart refused to believe it.

They leaned closer.

And for a brief moment—

The world disappeared.

## Blood and Strategy

The battlefield was exactly as Seraphina had feared.

The enemy army was three times larger.

A trap.

Caelan saw it immediately.

"We don't fight them directly," he ordered.

"We outthink them."

Under his command, the soldiers vanished into the forest.

Hidden. Silent.

Waiting.

And when the moment came—

They struck.

One by one, the enemy forces fell.

Half their army collapsed before they even understood what was happening.

But the enemy adapted quickly.

The forest turned into chaos.

Steel clashed.

Cries echoed.

Blood stained the ground.

And in the center of it all—

Caelan fought like a storm.

He did not stop.

He did not fall.

Even when wounded.

Even when outnumbered.

And when the battle finally ended—

He stood victorious.

But barely.

## The Prince Who Would Not Die

He returned to the palace—

Wounded. Exhausted. Alive.

King Varen watched him enter the court.

And for the first time—

His smile did not reach his eyes.

## A Love That Refused to Break

When Seraphina saw him—

She ran to him without hesitation.

Tears fell freely now.

"You're alive…" she whispered, holding him tightly.

"I told you," he said faintly.

"I would return."

## The Truth Begins

Days later—

As Caelan rested, the royal physician ordered him to remain in bed for a week.

Seraphina stayed by his side.

Constantly.

Silently.

Watching.

Waiting.

And then—

Her messenger returned.

What he revealed—

Changed everything.

"This war… was not real," he said quietly.

"It was arranged."

Her breath stopped.

"By the neighboring king… and—"

He hesitated.

"—King Varen."

## A Truth Rejected

That night, she told Caelan everything.

But he shook his head.

"No."

"You're wrong."

"She is not wrong," she insisted.

"Then why did he send you with so few soldiers?" she demanded.

"Why did he never treat you like family?"

Her voice trembled—not with fear—

But with truth.

"He raised you as a weapon," she said.

"Not as a son."

"That's enough," Caelan said sharply.

The room fell silent.

"You're connecting things that aren't there."

"And you are refusing to see what is right in front of you," she replied.

His expression hardened.

"Focus on us," he said coldly.

"Not on him."

## The Beginning of Truth

But Seraphina knew.

This was only the beginning.

The first attempt had failed.

The next one…

Would not.

Somewhere in the shadows—

King Varen stood alone.

"They still don't understand," he whispered.

His eyes darkened.

"So I will make them."

The truth is coming.

The prince is not ready.

But the princess… is.
