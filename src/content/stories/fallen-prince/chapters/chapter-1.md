# The Kingdom of Sunrise

The Kingdom of Aurelion was a land of golden mornings and peaceful nights, where hope lived in every home and laughter echoed through its streets.

At the heart of this kingdom stood its ruler—

King Aldric Valen.

He was not only a warrior of unmatched bravery but also a man of kindness and honor. Tall, strong, and strikingly handsome, King Aldric was admired by all—not just for his victories in battle, but for the warmth with which he ruled his people.

By his side stood Queen Elira Valen—a woman whose beauty was spoken of across distant lands. Yet it was her intelligence and wisdom that truly defined her. She was graceful, sharp-minded, and deeply devoted to both her kingdom and her family.

Together, they were loved.

Together, they were Aurelion.

And then there was their son…

Prince Caelan Valen—only two years old, with innocent eyes that had yet to see the darkness of the world.

One more member completed the royal family.

Lord Varen Valen, the king's younger brother.

A man who had never stood on a battlefield.

A man who avoided war… yet never avoided power.

## The Illness

It began without warning.

A sudden weakness.

A persistent cough.

A strange exhaustion that no rest could cure.

King Aldric, once the strongest man in the kingdom, began to fade.

The palace summoned the finest physicians from across the land, but none could understand the illness.

"There is no wound… no visible poison," one healer said, confused. "Yet his life is slipping away."

Queen Elira remained by his side day and night, refusing to leave.

"There must be a cure," she said firmly. "There must be something we have not tried."

Days passed.

Hope faded.

And then…

A new doctor arrived at the palace gates.

## The Doctor

It was Lord Varen who brought him.

"I have found someone who may help," Varen said calmly as the physician entered the royal chamber.

The man bowed low before the king and queen.

"There is… one possibility," he said slowly. "A rare herb known as Noctis Verdan."

Queen Elira stepped forward. "Where can it be found?"

"At the peaks of the Black Mountain Hills," the doctor replied. "But I must warn you… the mountain is deadly. Its air itself carries poison. Many who have gone there never returned."

The room fell silent.

"I will go," Elira said.

King Aldric tried to protest, his voice weak.

"No… I cannot lose you too."

She held his hand gently, her eyes filled with quiet strength.

"You won't," she whispered. "I will return."

## The Journey

At dawn, Queen Elira set out toward the Black Mountain with a group of trusted royal guards.

The gates of the palace closed behind them.

From the shadows of a high balcony…

Lord Varen watched.

And for a brief moment—

he smiled.

## The Trap

The journey was long and dangerous.

When they reached the Black Mountain Hills, a strange fog surrounded them.

At first, it seemed natural.

Then the guards began to cough.

One by one, they fell.

But it was no ordinary poison.

Hidden within the mist… unseen figures moved.

Men loyal to Lord Varen.

They had been waiting.

The queen and her guards had not walked into danger—

They had been led into it.

## The Fall of a King

Days later, a single horse returned to the palace.

Blood stained its saddle.

No riders.

No survivors.

The news spread like wildfire.

Queen Elira was dead.

When King Aldric heard the truth, his spirit shattered.

"Elira…" he whispered, his voice breaking.

Grief consumed what little strength he had left.

That very night…

the king of Aurelion took his final breath.

## A New King

The kingdom fell into mourning.

Their beloved king was gone.

Their queen… lost forever.

And only one heir remained—a child too young to even understand what had been taken from him.

Prince Caelan.

With no ruler to guide the kingdom, the throne could not remain empty.

And so, Lord Varen stepped forward.

"For the sake of Aurelion," he declared before the people, his face filled with controlled sorrow, "I will bear this responsibility."

Broken and desperate, the people accepted him.

They trusted him.

They had no reason not to.

## The Truth

But behind closed doors…

There was no grief.

Only silence.

And a quiet voice in the dark.

"It is done."

The illness that weakened the king.

The doctor who spoke of the herb.

The poison on the mountain.

None of it had been fate.

Every piece had been carefully placed.

By the man who now wore the crown.

## The Fallen Prince

In a quiet chamber of the palace, a child slept peacefully.

Unaware of the blood that had been spilled for the throne.

Unaware that his parents were gone.

Unaware… that the man protecting him was the one who destroyed his world.

Prince Caelan Valen.

The last true heir of Aurelion.

The boy who would one day rise…

As the Fallen Prince.
