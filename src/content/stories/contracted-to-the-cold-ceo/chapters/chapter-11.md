# Chapter 11: The Confrontation

The silence in the study was suffocating.

Lyra tried to yank her wrist back, but Alexander's grip was a wall of solid steel. His broad, muscular frame enveloped her completely, his thumb pressing firmly against her racing pulse.

"Let go of me," she whispered, her voice laced with a lethal mix of anger and betrayal.

"No," Alexander rasped, his deep voice carrying zero hesitation. "You can be furious with me, Lyra. You can tear this house apart. But I am not letting you step out that door."

"You built a cage around me!" Tears glistened in her dark eyes, but her chin remained lifted, her quiet grace transforming into raw, burning rebellion. "You watched my family collapse. You sat in your glass tower while my father lost everything, knowing you were going to use his desperation to buy me!"

Alexander's ice-gray eyes darkened, a muscle ticking in his sharp, angular jaw. He pulled her half an inch closer, forcing her to look directly into the storm in his eyes.

"Your father made deals with dangerous men long before I stepped in," he growled low, his breath hot against her skin. "If I hadn't bought his debt, someone far worse would have taken his press—and they wouldn't have put a contract on a desk, Lyra. They would have taken you by force."

"So you decided to play god instead?"

"I decided to protect what was mine!" The roar in his voice shattered the heavy quiet of the room, exposing the raw, feral possessiveness beneath his cold corporate exterior. "I saw you three years ago. You were soft, quiet, and completely untouched by the filth of this city. I knew the moment I looked at you that I would tear Manhattan down brick by brick before I let another man touch you."

Lyra's breath hitched. The sheer intensity of his obsession was staggering—a terrifying force that made her head spin and her heart hammer wildly against her ribs.

"I didn't force you into my bed last night," Alexander whispered, his voice dropping an octave, becoming a rough, ragged plea as his free hand rose to gently frame her face. His calloused thumb brushed away a single teardrop on her cheek. "I gave you every chance to walk away. But you didn't. Because you feel this just as deeply as I do."

"That doesn't make it right, Alexander," she breathed, her hands resting flat against his broad, warm chest, feeling the chaotic rhythm of his heart beneath his shirt.

"I don't care about right," he murmured, leaning down until his lips brushed against her ear. "I care about keeping you."

He reached into his pocket, pulled out the sleek leather folder containing their signed Indenture Agreement, and set it on the desk beside them. Before Lyra could react, Alexander picked up a silver lighter, clicked the flame to life, and held it to the corner of the paper.

Lyra gasped as the bright orange flame consumed the contract, reducing the legal debt of her family to black ash on the obsidian desk.

"There," Alexander rasped, his gray eyes locking onto hers as the fire died out. "The debt is gone. The contract is destroyed. You are completely free, Lyra."

He slowly released her wrist, stepping back to give her space for the first time since she had walked into the room.

"The car is downstairs," he said quietly, his voice devoid of warmth, though his posture remained taut with immense restraint. "If you want to leave... walk out."
