# Chapter 6: The Executive Floor

The sun hadn't even cleared the glass horizon of Manhattan when the sleek black sedan pulled up to the private underground entrance of Vance Tower.

Lyra sat in the back, dressed in a tailored charcoal knee-length skirt and a crisp white silk blouse—courtesy of the wardrobe Alexander's staff had placed in her room overnight. Her long dark hair was pinned up in a neat, professional chignon, though a few soft strands escaped to frame her pale face. She looked every bit the high-level executive assistant, but inside, her nerves were pulled taut as piano wire.

The elevator bypassed all seventy-four public floors, sweeping directly up to the executive suite.

When the doors slid open, Alexander was already there, standing at the head of a massive glass conference table surrounded by eight senior partners. He looked devastating—a dark three-piece suit fitted to his six-foot-three frame, dark hair brushed back, his razor-sharp jawline set in complete indifference as he tore down a multi-million-dollar acquisition proposal.

He didn't pause his presentation as Lyra stepped out, but his ice-gray hunter eyes tracked her every move across the polished marble floor.

"Adjust the valuation by four percent, gentlemen," Alexander commanded flatly, tossing a pen onto the glass. "If their board resists, buy their debt through the shell entity and liquidate them by Friday. Meeting adjourned."

The executives scrambled out of the room like men escaping a firing squad, leaving a heavy, charged silence behind them.

Lyra stood by the main desk, holding a leather tablet. "Good morning, Mr. Vance. I've reviewed your morning schedule."

Alexander walked slowly toward her, unbuttoning his suit jacket and placing his hands deep in his trouser pockets. His sheer size and presence dominated the space instantly.

"You're standing five feet away from me, Lyra," he murmured, his deep, gravelly voice dropping an octave as he stopped just inches from her desk. "Is that the new distance rule you set for yourself?"

"I am maintaining professional boundaries in the office," she said softly, keeping her gaze steady despite the sudden spike in her pulse. "Your 9:00 AM briefing with the regional directors begins in ten minutes."

Alexander leaned down, resting his palms flat on her desk, trapping her between his arms. The scent of rain, expensive cedar, and pure power engulfed her senses. His gray eyes burned into hers with zero intent of hiding his hunger.

"There are no boundaries in this office, little flower," he whispered, his thumb reaching out to brush along her jawline, tilting her chin up. "You work for me. You live with me. And every person on this floor knows you arrived in my private car."

Lyra's breath hitched, her fingers gripping the edge of the desk behind her as the sheer physical heat between them threatened to melt her quiet resolve. "Alexander, someone will walk in..."

"Let them," Alexander rasped, closing the distance until his lips brushed against hers in a slow, possessive warning. "Let them see exactly who owns this floor."
