# Chapter 5: The Unraveling

The heat between them was staggering.

Alexander's broad hand rested flat over Lyra's ribcage, his thumb stroking the soft curve of her waist as his mouth traced a path of unbearable tension down the sensitive line of her throat. Every touch made her skin burn, her chest rising and falling in shallow, frantic breaths.

"Alexander..." Her fingers tightened in his dark hair, a quiet, shuddering plea escaping her lips. "Wait..."

He stilled instantly.

For three agonized heartbeats, the only sound in the room was the heavy rain slashing against the glass wall and the sound of their ragged breathing. Alexander lifted his head slowly. His gray hunter eyes were dark, almost black with raw desire, but the lethal discipline that ran his empire took control.

His muscles remained corded with tension, his broad chest hovering inches above hers, but he didn't push further.

"Give me one reason to stop, Lyra," he rasped, his deep voice heavy and laced with a dangerous edge. "One reason that isn't a lie."

Lyra looked up into the intense, unyielding gaze of the most powerful man in the city. Her shy, quiet exterior had always been her shield, but here, pinned beneath him on charcoal silk, she felt completely bare.

"Because..." Her voice trembled, soft but steady as she forced herself to look him dead in the eyes. "Because I won't be just another transaction on your desk. You bought my debt, Alexander. You didn't buy my surrender."

A heavy silence stretched across the room.

Alexander stared at her, his jaw clenching so hard a muscle ticked beneath his sharp skin. For a second, Lyra thought he would ignore her—that his possessive nature would overpower his restraint.

Instead, Alexander let out a low, dark breath. He pushed himself up, swinging his long legs off the edge of the bed and sitting up. His broad back, lined with dense muscle and faint scars of a past he never talked about, flexed as he ran both hands through his dark hair.

"You think this is a game," he murmured, his voice cold once more, though the tension in his shoulders betrayed him. He stood up, towering beside the bed as he rebuttoned his shirt with steady, precise movements. "You think you're holding out for a choice."

Lyra sat up slowly, pulling her open silk blouse back over her shoulders, her hands still shaking from the aftershocks of his touch. "I signed the contract. I am here. Isn't that enough for tonight?"

Alexander turned, looking down at her with a look so sharp, so terrifyingly possessive, that it made her heart stop.

"It will never be enough," he said flatly.

He walked toward the adjoining dressing room, stopping at the threshold without turning around.

"Sleep, Lyra. Tomorrow morning at 7:00 AM, my driver takes you to the firm. You start your new position as my executive assistant. And if you think staying in my shadow ten hours a day is going to make this easier..." He glanced back over his shoulder, his gray eyes glinting in the dark. "...you are dead wrong."

The dressing room door closed, and Lyra sank back into the pillows, her pulse hammering against her ribs long into the night.
