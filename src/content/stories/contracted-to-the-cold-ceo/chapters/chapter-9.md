# Chapter 9: The Surrender

The rain was coming down hard by the time the Maybach pulled into the private courtyard of the estate.

Neither of them had spoken a word during the drive back from the gala, but the air inside the leather interior had been thick, suffocating, and charged with a dangerous, unspoken promise.

Alexander didn't wait for the driver to walk around. He opened his own door, stepped into the storm, and reached back in to offer his hand to Lyra. The moment her fingers touched his warm, calloused palm, he didn't just lead her—he pulled her flush against his side, his large umbrella shielding her emerald dress as they crossed the threshold into the dimly lit foyer.

The double doors closed behind them, shutting out the rest of the world.

Lyra barely had time to kick off her heels before Alexander's hands were on her waist. He lifted her effortlessly against the cool wood of the closed grand entryway, his heavy, six-foot-three frame pinning her completely.

"Alexander..." her voice was a breathy, breathless whisper in the shadowed foyer.

"No more contracts," Alexander growled low, his deep voice vibrating right against her chest. His ice-gray eyes burned down into hers with absolute, unfiltered hunger. "No more titles. No more debt."

His hands moved to the zipper at the back of her emerald gown. With one smooth, deliberate pull, the heavy velvet parted, pooling down to her hips. His rough palms slid directly onto the bare skin of her ribs, his thumbs tracing the curve of her waist. Lyra gasped at the sudden, fiery heat of his touch, her shy exterior completely evaporating as she arched into his palms.

"Say my name, Lyra," he rasped, his lips brushing the sensitive line beneath her ear, sending shivers trailing down her spine. "Not Mr. Vance. Say it."

"Alexander..." she breathed out, her hands reaching up to unbutton his tuxedo jacket, pushing the fine wool off his broad, corded shoulders until it fell to the marble floor. Her fingers slid into his dark hair, pulling him closer. "I'm not running."

That single sentence shattered the last thread of his restraint.

Alexander gathered her into his arms, carrying her up the sweeping staircase without breaking the electric gaze between them. He pushed through the double frosted-glass doors of the master suite, bringing her straight to the center of the massive king bed.

He laid her down gently on the charcoal silk sheets, his hovering frame enveloping her.

*(Scene fades as the night unfolds between them.)*

When morning came, wrapped in silk and quiet, Lyra surrendered completely to the man who held her heart, and Alexander held her as if she were the only thing in the world he couldn't afford to lose.
