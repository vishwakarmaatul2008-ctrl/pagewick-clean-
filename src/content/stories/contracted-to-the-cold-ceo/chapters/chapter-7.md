# Chapter 7: The Late-Night Shift

The seventy-fifth floor was completely dead by 9:30 PM.

The glowing lights of the city hummed through the floor-to-ceiling glass, casting long shadows across the empty obsidian floor. Lyra sat at her desk, her long dark hair falling over her shoulder as she compiled the final district acquisition files. Her eyes burned with exhaustion, but she refused to step down.

A soft shadow fell over her tablet.

Alexander stood beside her desk, his jacket long gone. His white dress shirt was unbuttoned at the top three buttons, rolling up to reveal the thick, corded muscle of his forearms. He carried the scent of cold rain, cedar, and raw, oppressive power.

"You should have been in the car an hour ago, little flower," Alexander rasped, his ice-gray hunter eyes burning down into her face.

"The filings needed to be finalized for the morning trade," Lyra said softly, her voice steady despite her racing pulse. She stood up, reaching to gather her coat. "I am done now."

Alexander didn't step back. Instead, he reached out, his broad hand catching her wrist gently but firmly, stopping her in her tracks.

"You aren't done," he whispered, his thumb slowly stroking the sensitive skin of her inner wrist. "You've been avoiding my eyes all day."

"I have been doing my job, Mr. Vance."

"Look at me."

Lyra lifted her chin, meeting his icy gaze. The air between them instantly thin, supercharged with an electric, suffocating heat that made her chest heave.

Without breaking eye contact, Alexander leaned down, wrapping his arm around her waist and effortlessly lifting her onto the smooth edge of her executive desk. He stepped right between her knees, trapping her completely against his massive, muscular frame.

"Alexander..." her breath hitched as his fingers slid into her dark hair, pulling out the pins until her long waves spilled down past her waist.

"You signed a contract to stay in my world, Lyra," he murmured against her lips, his hand dropping to unbutton the top button of her silk blouse. "Did you really think we were going to pretend this isn't happening every single night?"

"You said..." She gasped as his mouth pressed against the racing pulse point beneath her ear, "You said I had a choice."

"You do," Alexander growled low against her skin, his large hand cupping her waist, lifting her closer until there wasn't a single inch of space left between them. "Tell me to walk away right now, and I will."

Lyra's hands rested on his broad chest, feeling the hard, rapid beat of his heart beneath the fine fabric. Her mouth opened, but no words came out. The shy archivist inside her dissolved into the raw, intense fire burning through her veins.

Instead of pushing him away, her fingers tightened around his shirt collar, pulling him closer.
