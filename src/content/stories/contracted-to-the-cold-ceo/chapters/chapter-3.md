# Chapter 3: The Enclosure

The metallic click of the pen echoed through the silent office like a gunshot.

Lyra's hand trembled slightly as she pressed the nib to the paper, signing her full name on the dotted line. Lyra Mercer. Two words that effectively ended her freedom and traded away the next year of her life.

Alexander watched her with sharp, predatory focus. The moment the ink dried, his long fingers reached down and pulled the contract away, sliding it into a black leather portfolio without a second glance.

"A sensible decision," he said, his deep voice carrying a cool, quiet satisfaction. He stepped back, reaching for his suit jacket from the floor and sliding his broad shoulders into it with effortless grace. "Your belongings at your current apartment are already being packed and transferred."

Lyra's head snapped up, her long dark hair cascading over her shoulders. "What? I haven't even gone back to pack!"

"You don't need to," Alexander replied smoothly, adjusting his silver cufflinks. "Anything worth keeping will meet you at the estate. The rest will be discarded and replaced."

He reached into his inner pocket, pulled out a heavy black keycard, and tossed it onto the desk in front of her.

"My driver is waiting in the private underground bay," he commanded, stepping closer until his shadow completely enveloped her once again. He reached out, his warm, calloused fingers lifting her chin one last time, his ice-gray eyes burning down into hers. "Go down, get in the car, and wait for me. If you attempt to step outside the garage gates, the agreement is voided—and your father's arrest warrant goes live."

Lyra swallowed the lump in her throat, her shy exterior holding back a wave of fiery rebellion. She grabbed the keycard, her fingers brushing against his warm palm for a fraction of a second, sending an unwanted spark straight up her arm.

Without another word, she turned and walked toward the heavy oak doors.

Twenty minutes later, the sleek, blacked-out sedan glided out of the tower's private subterranean gate and onto the rain-drenched streets of the city. Lyra sat in the plush leather backseat, her hands folded tightly in her lap as she watched the glowing neon lights of Manhattan blur past the tinted glass.

The car didn't stop in the city. It headed north, climbing into the quiet, heavily wooded hills where the ultra-wealthy built their isolated fortresses.

Finally, the vehicle slowed down, turning into a massive iron gate flanked by high stone walls. The gates swung open silently, revealing a sprawling, modern architectural estate carved directly into the cliffside. High glass walls, dark marble pillars, and warm exterior accent lights cut through the rain.

The driver opened her door, holding a large black umbrella overhead. "Welcome to the Vance Residence, Miss Mercer."

Lyra stepped out, her worn shoes silent on the wet stone driveway. As she crossed the threshold into the towering, two-story foyer, a maid stepped forward, taking her damp coat.

"Mr. Vance requested that you be shown straight to the master suite," the woman said respectfully, gesturing toward the floating grand staircase.

Lyra's heart skipped a beat. "The... master suite? Surely there is a guest room?"

"Mr. Vance does not keep guest rooms in his primary residence, Miss," the maid replied calmly. "Please, follow me."

Her chest tightened as she climbed the stairs. At the end of the long, dimly lit hallway, the maid pushed open double frosted-glass doors, revealing a massive suite overlooking the stormy forest outside. In the center of the room sat a king-sized bed lined with charcoal silk sheets, and through an adjoining doorway, a freestanding marble tub sat steam-filled and waiting.

Before Lyra could step further into the room, the heavy front doors downstairs slammed shut.

Heavy, slow footsteps echoed up the grand staircase. Alexander was home.
