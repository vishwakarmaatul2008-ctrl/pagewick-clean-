# Chapter 12: Sovereign

The pile of black ash rested on the obsidian desk between them, a quiet, smoldering mark of everything that had been destroyed.

Alexander turned toward the glass wall, his broad back facing her. His corded shoulders were rigid, the dense muscles under his skin pulled taut as he waited for the heavy click of the study door—the sound that would signal her departure. For the first time in his life, the man who owned half of Manhattan was entirely powerless.

Lyra looked at the ash, then at the towering, six-foot-three silhouette standing by the window.

The black leather sedan was waiting downstairs. Her family was solvent. Her father was safe. She had her freedom, complete and unburdened, handed back to her on a silver platter.

She took a slow, deliberate step forward.

Her soft bare feet made no sound against the rug as she crossed the floor, closing the distance between them. She didn't head for the exit. Instead, she stopped right behind him, reaching out until her small, delicate palms rested flat against his warm, broad back.

Alexander stilled instantly, his breath hitching.

"I hated the contract, Alexander," Lyra whispered, her voice carrying a quiet, unwavering strength that filled the vast room. "I hated feeling trapped. But I never hated you."

Alexander spun around, his broad hands reaching out instantly to frame her face. His ice-gray hunter eyes were raw, dark, and filled with a rare, dangerous vulnerability.

"You should run from me, Lyra," he rasped, his deep voice thick with restraint as his thumb stroked her cheekbone. "I am possessive, relentless, and I will never let another man come near you."

"I know," Lyra said softly, a small, breathless smile touching her lips. She reached up, her soft hands covering his calloused ones. "I am not staying because of a paper agreement. I am staying because I choose you."

Alexander let out a ragged, dark breath, pulling her into a fierce, breathless kiss that obliterated every lingering trace of doubt.

It wasn't a punishment—it was a total, mutual surrender. His arm wrapped around her waist, lifting her off the ground as she wrapped her arms around his neck, her fingers tangling deep into his dark hair.

He carried her to the desk, sweeping the empty files aside and setting her down gently on the dark wood, his lips finding hers again and again between whispered words neither of them wanted to stop saying.

"You are my sovereign, Lyra," Alexander murmured against her lips, his heavy chest heaving against hers as he looked down at her with unfiltered, eternal devotion. "Not my debtor. My equal. My wife."

Lyra pulled his sharp jawline down to hers, her dark eyes shining with intense, fiery devotion. "Then prove it, Mr. Vance."

*(Scene fades as morning light spills across the room.)*

Outside, the morning sun finally broke through the clouds, bathing the executive penthouse in golden light. The debt was settled, the contract was gone, and the sovereign had finally met his match.
