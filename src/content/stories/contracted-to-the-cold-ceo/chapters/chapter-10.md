# Chapter 10: The Secret Exposed

Morning sunlight filtered through the sheer drapery of the master suite, casting long, golden streaks across the charcoal silk sheets.

Lyra woke slowly, the lingering warmth of Alexander's presence still clinging to the room. She reached across the mattress, but the space beside her was empty. The lingering scent of cedar, crisp cotton, and cold rain told her he hadn't been gone long.

She sat up, pulling a plush black silk robe over her shoulders and knotting it around her waist. The estate was dead silent.

Walking out into the hallway, she noticed the heavy oak doors to Alexander's private study were standing slightly ajar. Curiosity pulling at her, Lyra pushed the door open quietly.

The vast room was empty, illuminated by the soft morning light. In the center sat his massive obsidian desk. Resting near the corner was an antique mahogany valet box—unlocked and open, its hidden lower drawer pulled out just an inch.

Lyra hesitated, her heart beating a fast rhythm against her ribs. She stepped closer, her eyes dropping to the thick leather folder sitting inside the velvet compartment.

Stamped across the front cover in dark ink was her own name: LYRA MERCER.

Her fingers trembled as she lifted the folder, opening it to the first page. She expected legal documents, land deeds, or financial ledgers. Instead, her breath hitched in her throat.

Inside was a precise, detailed dossier spanning back three years.

There were candid photographs of her—cataloging old manuscripts at the university library, walking home in the rain, sitting at a coffee shop near her old apartment. Below the photos were handwritten notes detailing her schedules, her father's accumulating debts, and the exact financial moves Alexander had made to systematically buy up every single loan her family owed.

The debt hadn't been a sudden corporate acquisition. He had engineered the entire financial trap long before she ever set foot in Vance Tower.

"You weren't supposed to find that, little flower."

Alexander's deep, rasping voice echoed from the doorway.

Lyra spun around, the folder slipping from her hands and scattering pages across the floor. He stood in the frame, dressed in a black tailored shirt with the sleeves rolled to his forearms, holding two cups of coffee. His ice-gray hunter eyes were locked onto her, completely unreadable, masking a dangerous, brewing tension.

"You tracked me," Lyra whispered, her voice cracking as the betrayal cut straight through her. "For three years... you orchestrated my father's bankruptcy. You made sure I had nowhere else to go."

Alexander set the coffee down on the side table, taking two slow, heavy strides into the room until he stood directly in front of her.

"I didn't cause your father's failure, Lyra," he said, his tone low, quiet, and possessing a terrifying calm. "He ruined himself. I simply ensured that when he fell, I was the one holding the safety net."

"You bought my life!" She stepped back, her dark eyes flashing with a mixture of heartbreak and defiance. "This whole time... the contract, the office, this house... it was all planned?"

"Yes," Alexander rasped without a second of hesitation, his hand reaching out to grab her wrist before she could move further away. He pulled her flush against his broad chest, his grip firm, dark, and unyielding. "I saw you three years ago at the archive gala, and I decided right then that you belonged to me. I took the only path that guaranteed you would end up in my world."

Lyra stared at him, her chest heaving as the raw, possessive truth of his obsession lay completely exposed between them.
