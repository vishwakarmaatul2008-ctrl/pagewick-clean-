# Chapter 4: The Sovereign's Right

The heavy double doors of the master suite clicked shut behind Lyra, sealing her inside the cavernous space.

The room was bathed in the low, ambient glow of floor-level accent lights. Rain rattled violently against the glass wall overlooking the dark canopy of the valley, but inside, the air was warm, quiet, and suffocatingly still.

Lyra stood frozen at the edge of the plush charcoal rug, her fingers clutching the delicate fabric of her unbuttoned silk blouse. Every sense was heightened—listening for the heavy, rhythmic tread of footsteps coming up the hallway.

The door handle turned.

Alexander stepped into the room. He had already shed his dark tie and suit jacket, leaving his white dress shirt unbuttoned at the collar, exposing the corded muscles of his throat. His sleeves were rolled tightly past his forearms, dark hair slightly disheveled from the storm outside.

His ice-gray hunter eyes locked onto her instantly, tracking her location in the room like a predator locating its target.

"You're still fully dressed," Alexander rasped, his deep voice cutting through the ambient sound of the rain.

"I told your maid there has been a mistake," Lyra said softly, her chin lifting as she forced her voice to stay steady. "I signed a debt consolidation contract, Alexander. I did not agree to share a bed with you."

Alexander slowly crossed the room, his long strides closing the distance until he was standing directly in front of her. The sheer height difference forced her to tilt her head back to meet his gaze. The scent of rain, cedar, and raw, unrestrained power rolled off him in waves.

"Read clause seven again, little flower," he whispered, stepping forward until her back met the cold marble pillar near the bed frame. "You transferred your full administrative and personal equity to me. That includes where you sleep... and who you sleep with."

He reached out, his long, calloused fingers wrapping firmly around her waist, pulling her flush against his solid, broad frame. Lyra gasped, the sudden warmth of his body burning straight through her thin clothes.

"Alexander..." her breath hitched as his other hand came up, his thumb tracing the soft, flushed skin of her cheek before slid down to grip her chin, tilting her lips upward.

"You want to pretend this is purely financial," he growled against her mouth, his eyes dark with an unholy, possessive intensity. "So tell me to stop. Tell me you don't feel this every time I touch you."

Lyra's lips parted, but no words came out. The sheer chemistry between them was overwhelming—a violent, electric current that made her pulse race and her knees turn to water.

Before she could form a reply, Alexander dipped his head, capturing her mouth in a deep, punishing kiss.

It wasn't gentle. It was a complete takeover. His tongue demanded entry, tasting her deeply as his large hand moved down her spine, arching her body into his. Lyra let out a soft, helpless whimper, her hands instinctively rising to grip the front of his open shirt, her fingers tangling in the fabric as she surrendered to the fire consuming her.

Alexander groaned low in his chest, lifting her effortlessly by her thighs and setting her down onto the silk sheets of the massive bed. He immediately hovered over her, his six-foot-three muscular frame trapping her completely beneath him.

Without breaking the intense gaze of his gray eyes, his hands reached for the buttons of her blouse, flicking them open one by one until the silk parted, exposing her smooth, porcelain skin to the warm room air.

"You belong to this estate now, Lyra," he murmured, his lips trailing a line of fiery kisses down her exposed neck to her collarbone, making her arch into his touch. "And tonight, I collect the first payment."
