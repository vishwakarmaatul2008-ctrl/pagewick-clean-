# Chapter 5 — The Room at the End

Maya didn't open the door.

Not that night.

She returned to her bedroom, locked the door, and pushed the desk against it.

It was ridiculous.

She knew that.

But after everything that had happened, she wasn't interested in being brave.

She wanted morning.

She wanted sunlight.

She wanted to leave Blackwood House and never come back.

Yet sleep never came.

At 3:17 a.m., Maya heard footsteps in the hallway.

Slow.

Barely audible.

They stopped outside her door.

Then came three knocks.

Knock.

Knock.

Knock.

Maya held her breath.

A voice whispered from the other side.

Her mother's voice.

"You promised."

Maya covered her ears.

"You're not real."

Silence.

Then the footsteps moved away.

Maya waited.

One minute.

Two.

Five.

Finally, she opened the door.

The hallway was empty.

But something was sitting on the floor.

A small wooden box.

Maya immediately recognized it.

It was the box from Evelyn's photograph.

She picked it up.

There was no lock.

Inside was a silver key.

And beneath it---

a photograph.

Maya turned it over.

Her heart stopped.

It showed her mother.

Not in 2003.

Not eleven years ago.

It showed her mother standing in the exact same hallway Maya was standing in now.

Behind her was the forbidden room.

The photograph was dated:

June 17, 2014.

Maya stared at it.

Her mother had supposedly been dead for three years by then.

"No..."

Someone had been lying to her.

But who?

---

Maya walked to the end of the hallway.

The silver key felt cold in her hand.

She stood before the locked door.

For several seconds, she simply stared at it.

Then she inserted the key.

Click.

The lock opened.

Maya pushed the door.

It didn't move.

She pushed harder.

The door slowly opened with a deep groan.

A room appeared behind it.

It was surprisingly small.

There was a bed.

A wooden table.

An old mirror.

And dozens of photographs covering the walls.

Maya stepped inside.

Her eyes moved across them.

Evelyn.

Her mother.

The same house.

The same hallway.

Different years.

2003\.

2004\.

2005\.

2006\.

2007\.

2008\.

2009\.

Some photographs showed people Maya didn't recognize.

Women.

Always women.

And every woman wore the same silver necklace.

Maya touched her own bare neck.

The necklace was missing.

She looked down.

It was lying on the floor behind her.

She hadn't felt it fall.

Maya slowly picked it up.

Then she saw something written on the wall.

A single sentence.

THE HOUSE DOES NOT CHOOSE THE GIRL.

Below it, another sentence had been scratched into the wood.

THE GIRL CHOOSES THE HOUSE.

Maya frowned.

"What does that mean?"

A soft sound came from the mirror.

Maya looked up.

Her reflection was standing there.

But Maya wasn't.

The reflection slowly raised its hand.

And pointed behind her.

Maya turned.

Nothing.

She looked back at the mirror.

Her reflection was still pointing.

This time, Maya noticed something in the reflection.

Someone was standing behind her.

Evelyn.

Maya spun around.

Nothing.

When she looked back---

Evelyn was gone.

But the mirror had changed.

Words were appearing across the glass.

One letter at a time.

M A Y A

Then:

Y O U C A M E B A C K

Maya stepped away.

"No."

The mirror cracked.

A photograph fell from the wall.

Maya picked it up.

It showed her mother and Evelyn standing together.

Both were wearing the necklace.

On the back was a handwritten note.

Clara promised to return.

Maya's eyes filled with tears.

"Return from where?"

A voice answered.

"From here."

Maya froze.

The elderly woman stood in the doorway.

The same woman from the hallway.

Maya turned toward her.

"You knew my mother."

The woman nodded.

"You knew Evelyn."

Another nod.

"What is this place?"

The woman looked around the room.

"It isn't a room."

Maya frowned.

"What?"

"It's a doorway."

"To where?"

The woman's eyes filled with fear.

"To the place where the missing ones are kept."

Maya's heart pounded.

"Are they alive?"

The woman didn't answer.

Maya stepped toward her.

"Are they alive?"

The woman finally whispered:

"Some of them."

Maya's breath caught.

"My mother?"

The woman looked directly at her.

"Your mother came here for Evelyn."

"And?"

"She found her."

Maya waited.

"What happened?"

The woman looked toward the mirror.

"Your mother made a deal."

"What deal?"

Before the woman could answer, the mirror suddenly shattered.

Glass exploded across the floor.

Maya screamed and covered her face.

When she looked up, the elderly woman was gone.

The room was empty.

Except for a single photograph lying among the broken glass.

Maya picked it up.

Her hands began trembling.

It was a photograph of her mother.

And Evelyn.

Standing beside the same mirror.

But there was something else in the picture.

A little girl.

Maybe six years old.

Standing between them.

Maya stared at the child.

She knew that face.

She had seen it thousands of times.

In old family photographs.

In the mirror.

In her own memories.

It was her.

Maya dropped the photograph.

"No..."

A final message appeared on the broken mirror.

Not written in blood.

Not scratched into the glass.

Just ordinary black letters.

YOU WERE NEVER COMING TO BLACKWOOD HOUSE FOR THE FIRST TIME.

Maya couldn't breathe.

Then, from somewhere behind the walls---

a little girl's voice whispered:

"We have been waiting for you, Maya."
