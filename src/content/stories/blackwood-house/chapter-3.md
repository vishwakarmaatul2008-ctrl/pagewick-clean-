# Chapter 3 — The Girl in the Photograph

Maya didn't sleep that night.

She tried.

For nearly an hour, she lay beneath the heavy blankets in the guest bedroom, staring at the ceiling while rain scratched against the windows.

But every time she closed her eyes, she saw the photograph.

June 17, 2014.

SHE HAS BEEN WAITING FOR YOU.

Eventually, Maya sat up.

"Enough."

She switched on the bedside lamp.

The room looked ordinary.

Bed.

Wardrobe.

Small writing desk.

A mirror.

Nothing strange.

Nothing supernatural.

She almost felt embarrassed by how frightened she had become.

Maybe someone had placed the photographs there as a prank.

Maybe the property agent knew about some old family story.

Maybe---

Her eyes moved toward the mirror.

She froze.

For half a second, she thought she saw someone standing behind her.

A girl.

Long dark hair.

White dress.

Maya turned around.

Nothing.

She looked back at the mirror.

Only herself.

She exhaled slowly.

"You're losing it."

Then she noticed something.

The necklace.

Her mother's necklace was glowing faintly.

Not brightly.

Just enough to reflect a strange silver light against her collarbone.

Maya grabbed the pendant.

It was warm.

She pulled the chain over her head and placed it on the table.

The glow disappeared.

Maya stared at it.

Then she heard a sound from the hallway.

A soft knock.

Knock.

She didn't move.

Another.

Knock.

This time, from her bedroom door.

Maya slowly stood.

She walked toward it.

"Who's there?"

No answer.

She opened the door.

The hallway was empty.

But something was lying on the floor.

A small envelope.

Her name was written across it.

MAYA.

She picked it up.

Inside was a single photograph.

Maya's stomach tightened.

It was Evelyn.

But this photograph was different.

Evelyn wasn't standing in front of the house.

She was sitting on the floor of a bedroom.

Beside her was a small wooden box.

And around her neck---

Maya's necklace.

Maya turned the photograph over.

There were four words.

She wasn't the first.

Maya's blood ran cold.

"What does that mean?"

A voice answered from behind her.

"Because you weren't the first."

Maya screamed and spun around.

An elderly woman stood at the end of the hallway.

Maya had never seen her before.

The woman wore a long gray dress and held a candle in one hand.

Her face was pale and tired.

But her eyes were sharp.

"Who are you?"

The woman didn't answer.

Instead, she looked at the photograph in Maya's hand.

"You found her."

"Found who?"

"Evelyn."

Maya stepped backward.

"You know her?"

The woman nodded.

"I knew her."

"What happened to her?"

The woman's expression changed.

Fear.

Real fear.

"She disappeared."

"When?"

"Eleven years ago."

Maya stared at her.

"June 17, 2014?"

The woman slowly nodded.

Maya felt a chill run through her.

"What happened that night?"

The woman looked toward the end of the hallway.

Toward the locked room.

"She went inside."

Maya followed her gaze.

"The room?"

The woman nodded.

"And she never came out."

Silence filled the hallway.

Maya looked at the locked door.

"Then why did you say I wasn't the first?"

The woman looked directly into Maya's eyes.

"Because Evelyn wasn't the first girl to wear that necklace."

Maya's fingers tightened around the photograph.

"Who was?"

The woman opened her mouth.

But before she could answer---

the lights went out.

The candle in the woman's hand flickered.

Then died.

Darkness swallowed the hallway.

Maya couldn't see anything.

"Where are you?"

No answer.

"Hello?"

Silence.

Then---

A girl's voice whispered directly beside her ear.

"My mother."

Maya stumbled backward.

The lights suddenly returned.

The elderly woman was gone.

Maya stood alone.

She looked down at the photograph.

The picture had changed.

Evelyn was no longer sitting alone.

There was another woman beside her.

An older woman.

And Maya recognized her immediately.

It was her mother.

Maya's hands began shaking.

"No..."

She turned the photograph over.

The writing had changed too.

There were now five words.

Ask your mother about Blackwood House.

Maya stared at the sentence.

Her mother had died eight years ago.

She couldn't ask her.

Or could she?

A thought suddenly struck her.

Maya ran back into her room and grabbed her phone.

The screen finally turned on.

There was one new notification.

Unknown Number

Maya opened it.

One message.

No text.

Only a photograph.

Her mother's photograph.

Standing in front of Blackwood House.

And beneath it was a date.

June 17, 2003.

Eleven years before Evelyn disappeared.

Maya stared at the screen.

Her mother had been there.

Eleven years before Evelyn.

And suddenly, the sentence from the photograph made sense.

She wasn't the first.

Maya slowly looked toward the locked room at the end of the hallway.

For the first time, she understood.

The house wasn't waiting for someone to arrive.

It was waiting for her.

And whatever had happened to her mother---

had started here.
