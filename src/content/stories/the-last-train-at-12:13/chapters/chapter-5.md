# Chapter 5 — The Choice

The train doors began to close.

Arjun still held Anika's wrist.

"You're not going anywhere."

Anika stared at him.

"You don't understand."

"Then explain."

"I already did."

"No," Arjun said. "You remembered what happened. That's not the same as knowing the truth."

The train gave a low mechanical groan.

Inside, the four children remained seated.

His younger self was still pointing at him.

Him.

The station clock showed:

12:21 AM.

Three minutes remained.

Joseph suddenly stepped forward.

"Arjun."

Arjun looked at him.

"You need to let her go."

"Why?"

Joseph's expression was exhausted.

"Because I remember now."

He looked toward the train.

"I remember everything."

---

Twenty Years Earlier

It had been raining that night.

Five children had been waiting at Northfield Station.

Arjun.

Meera.

Rohan.

Joseph.

And Anika.

They hadn't known each other.

Not really.

They had all arrived with their families for different reasons.

But at 12:13 AM, the station changed.

The lights went out.

A train appeared on a track that hadn't been used for decades.

Its doors opened.

A voice came through the station speakers.

"Five passengers."

The children were frightened.

Anika's mother had been there.

She knew what the train was.

She had seen it once before.

And she knew its rule.

The train needed five passengers.

If five entered---

it would take them.

If four entered---

it would return for the fifth.

Anika's mother made a decision.

She pushed the other four children onto the train.

Then she grabbed Anika.

"Run!"

But Anika didn't run.

She saw the train doors closing.

She saw the other children crying.

So she pushed her mother away.

And stepped onto the platform.

The train left.

The four children disappeared.

Anika remained.

Her mother screamed.

But the station had already made its choice.

It took Anika instead.

The next morning, the four children were found alive.

But they remembered nothing.

Anika was never found.

---

Arjun stared at Joseph.

"You were there."

Joseph nodded.

"I was twelve."

"Then why do you remember?"

"Because I came back."

Arjun's face tightened.

"Why?"

Joseph looked at Anika.

"Because I promised her."

Anika whispered:

"You forgot."

Joseph closed his eyes.

"I know."

The train horn sounded.

12:22 AM.

Two minutes.

The four children inside the train began whispering.

All of them together.

"Someone must stay."

Anika pulled her wrist free.

She stepped toward the train.

Arjun grabbed her again.

"No."

She looked at him.

"Arjun, listen to me."

"I am listening."

"If I don't go, the train will take someone else."

"Then let it take me."

Anika froze.

Joseph shouted:

"No!"

Arjun looked at him.

"Why?"

Joseph's eyes filled with fear.

"Because that's what it wants."

The train doors opened again.

Inside, his younger self stood.

The child walked toward the doorway.

He looked directly at Arjun.

"You always choose wrong."

Arjun's heart sank.

"Who are you?"

The child smiled.

"You."

Then the child's face changed.

For one second, it became older.

It became Arjun's face.

Then older again.

Then Joseph's.

Then Meera's.

Then Rohan's.

Then Anika's.

The face kept changing.

Arjun finally understood.

The train wasn't carrying the children.

It was carrying their memories.

And it had been feeding on them for twenty years.

He looked at the four people beside him.

"That's why we forgot."

Nobody answered.

Arjun stepped toward the train.

Anika grabbed him.

"Don't."

He looked at her.

"I'm not going inside."

He turned toward the broken floor.

"I'm going underneath."

Joseph's eyes widened.

"What?"

Arjun pointed toward the tunnel.

"The train isn't the door."

He looked at the old station.

"The station is."

The clock clicked.

12:23 AM.

One minute.

The five voices began screaming.

Arjun ran toward the broken floor.

Meera followed.

Then Rohan.

Joseph hesitated.

Anika looked at the train.

Her younger self was waiting.

Then she ran after them.

All five stood beside the opening.

Below them was a tunnel filled with old railway tracks.

But there was something else.

Hundreds of names had been carved into the walls.

Some were decades old.

Some were fresh.

And at the very end---

five names were written together.

ARJUN

MEERA

ROHAN

JOSEPH

ANIKA

Below them was a single sentence:

THE FIFTH MUST CHOOSE.

Arjun stared at it.

"What does that mean?"

Anika slowly shook her head.

"I think..."

She looked at the train.

"...I've been choosing every twenty years."

The train whistle screamed.

12:24 AM.

The station lights exploded.

Darkness swallowed everything.

Then Arjun heard a voice beside him.

His mother's voice.

"Arjun."

He turned.

She was standing at the edge of the tunnel.

Smiling.

"Come home."

Arjun's eyes filled with tears.

He knew she wasn't real.

But the voice was perfect.

Then she whispered:

"Your sister is waiting."

Arjun stepped forward.

And behind him, the train doors opened.

Four children screamed his name.

Anika suddenly shouted:

"ARJUN, DON'T LOOK BACK!"

He stopped.

The voice of his mother changed.

It became something deeper.

Something ancient.

"You already did."

A hand grabbed Arjun's shoulder.

And the entire station disappeared.

12:25 AM.

The last thing Arjun heard was the sound of a train leaving the station.

And one final announcement:

"Four passengers remaining."
