# Chapter 6 – Eight Feet

The floorboard lifted another inch.

Ren stumbled backward.

His grandmother grabbed his arm.

"Don't look underneath."

But Ren could already see darkness between the boards.

Something moved beneath the house.

Not crawling.

Standing.

Ren's breath caught.

The space beneath the house wasn't tall enough for a person to stand.

Yet something was standing there.

A hand slowly appeared between the wooden boards.

Long fingers.

Pale skin.

Then the hand disappeared.

Silence.

Ren's grandmother whispered:

"Your grandfather made the same mistake."

"What mistake?"

"He opened the floor."

Ren stared at her.

"What did he find?"

She looked at the photographs scattered across the desk.

"Her."

---

The floor suddenly slammed shut.

BANG!

Ren jumped.

The house became completely silent.

Then something knocked from underneath.

Tok.

Ren's grandmother began crying.

"She's awake."

"Who?"

"The woman."

Ren looked at the photographs again.

His grandfather had spent years documenting her.

There were dozens of pictures.

But one photograph was different.

It showed the house from outside.

The woman stood beside it.

Ren looked carefully.

Her head was above the roof.

He compared her height to the house.

Eight feet.

Maybe more.

Then he noticed something written beneath the photograph:

SHE IS NOT THE GHOST.

Ren frowned.

"What does that mean?"

His grandmother didn't answer.

He turned the photograph over.

On the back, his grandfather had written:

The woman is only the door.

Ren felt a chill.

"A door to what?"

His grandmother finally looked at him.

"I don't know."

A loud crash came from upstairs.

They both froze.

Then another.

BANG!

Ren grabbed the nearest flashlight.

"Stay here."

His grandmother caught his sleeve.

"Don't go upstairs."

"It's coming from my room."

"That's why."

Another crash.

Ren slowly climbed the stairs.

One step.

Two.

Three.

The hallway was empty.

His bedroom door was open.

Inside, everything looked normal.

Except for the photograph.

The one showing Ren standing beside the villagers.

It was lying on his bed.

Ren picked it up.

The date had changed.

It now read:

AUGUST 17, 2026.

Tomorrow.

Ren stared at the photograph.

The woman was standing behind him.

But this time she wasn't smiling.

She was pointing toward something.

Ren looked at where her finger pointed.

A second version of himself stood at the far end of the photograph.

Older.

Pale.

And completely alone.

Ren's heart began racing.

Then he noticed the most terrifying detail.

The second Ren was standing beside the same locked door.

And written above him were four words:

HE OPENED THE DOOR.

A voice whispered behind him.

"Ren."

He froze.

He didn't turn.

The voice continued.

"You've already opened it."

Ren slowly looked down.

The floor beneath his feet had changed.

The wooden boards were gone.

In their place was a black opening.

A staircase descended into darkness.

There had never been a basement beneath the house.

Ren knew that.

Yet the staircase was real.

And from somewhere below came the familiar sound:

Po... po... po...

His grandmother screamed from downstairs.

"REN! DON'T GO DOWN!"

Ren stared into the darkness.

Something was waiting below.

Then a woman's hand appeared on the first step.

She climbed slowly upward.

One step.

Then another.

Her head finally appeared.

Ren couldn't breathe.

She was taller than the doorway.

Her face was expressionless.

Her eyes were fixed on him.

Then she smiled.

And whispered:

"Your grandfather is still waiting."

Ren looked behind her.

Deep beneath the house, someone was standing in the darkness.

An old man.

He looked exactly like the photograph.

Ren whispered:

"Grandfather?"

The old man raised one finger to his lips.

Be quiet.

Then he pointed behind Ren.

Ren slowly turned.

His grandmother was standing in the doorway.

But she wasn't alone.

Something impossibly tall was standing directly behind her.

Ren's grandmother looked at him with tears in her eyes.

"Ren..."

She whispered:

"That's not me."
