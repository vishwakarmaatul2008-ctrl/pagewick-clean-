# Chapter 1 – The Sound

The first thing Ren noticed about the village was how quiet it was.

Not peaceful.

Quiet.

The kind of quiet that made every small sound feel too loud.

His grandmother's old house stood at the end of a narrow road surrounded by cedar trees. Ren had come to stay there for the summer after his parents decided that he needed "a break from the city."

He didn't mind.

There was no traffic. No crowded trains. No notifications every few seconds.

Just wind moving through the trees.

And, occasionally, the sound of someone walking outside.

Ren unpacked his bag before sunset.

The house was older than he expected. Wooden floors creaked beneath his feet, and the paper doors had yellowed with age. His grandmother had left several rooms untouched after moving to the city years ago.

One room was at the back of the house.

Its door was locked.

Ren noticed it while carrying his suitcase upstairs.

"What's in there?"

His grandmother didn't answer immediately.

Then she smiled.

"Nothing you need."

That answer bothered him more than it should have.

That night, Ren couldn't sleep.

Rain tapped against the roof.

He checked his phone.

2:16 AM.

Then---

Tok.

He froze.

A knock.

Not on the front door.

It had come from somewhere inside the house.

Ren sat up.

Silence.

Then---

Tok.

Another knock.

He stepped into the hallway.

The darkness seemed deeper downstairs.

"Grandma?"

No answer.

Ren slowly walked toward the staircase.

Tok.

This time it came from behind him.

He turned.

Nothing.

Only the locked door at the end of the hallway.

Ren stared at it.

Then he heard something that made his stomach tighten.

A voice.

Very faint.

From behind the door.

A woman's voice.

Almost a whisper.

"Po... po... po..."

Ren couldn't understand what it meant.

He moved closer.

The sound stopped.

He reached for the door handle.

Before his fingers touched it---

His grandmother suddenly grabbed his wrist.

Ren nearly screamed.

"Don't."

Her face was completely different now.

No smile.

No sleepiness.

Just fear.

"Don't open that door."

"Why?"

She looked toward the door.

Then she whispered:

"Because if you hear her again..."

She paused.

"...don't look outside."

Ren stared at her.

"Who?"

His grandmother didn't answer.

She simply pulled him away from the door.

And as they walked downstairs, Ren heard something from outside the house.

A strange sound.

Far away.

But getting closer.

Po... po... po...

Ren looked toward the window.

His grandmother immediately covered his eyes.

"Don't."

The sound stopped.

Ren waited.

Nothing.

Then his grandmother whispered:

"She's here."
