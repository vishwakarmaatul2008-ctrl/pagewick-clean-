# Chapter 4 – The Photograph

"Run."

Ren didn't question her.

His grandmother pulled him through the kitchen and toward the back door.

Behind them---

Click.

The bedroom door opened.

Ren heard a soft laugh.

Neither of them looked back.

They reached the kitchen.

His grandmother unlocked the back door with shaking hands.

Then she stopped.

There was something written on the wooden door.

Words scratched into the surface.

SHE KNOWS YOUR NAME.

Ren stared at it.

"Grandma..."

"Don't read it."

"But---"

"Don't."

She opened the door.

The backyard was empty.

Moonlight covered the grass.

For a moment, Ren thought they were safe.

Then he noticed something.

There were footprints in the wet soil.

Bare feet.

They began at the edge of the forest...

and ended directly beneath his bedroom window.

There were no footprints leading away.

His grandmother grabbed his arm.

"Don't look at the forest."

Ren looked anyway.

Something was standing between the trees.

Too tall.

Too still.

He couldn't see its face.

Only the outline of a woman whose head disappeared into the darkness above the trees.

Ren blinked.

She was gone.

---

They ran to the village shrine.

The old shopkeeper was already there.

When he saw Ren, his face changed.

"You saw her."

Ren nodded.

The old man looked at his grandmother.

"You should have told him sooner."

"I was trying to protect him."

"You can't protect someone from a thing that already knows their name."

Ren stepped forward.

"What does she want?"

Neither adult answered.

The old man took the photograph from his pocket.

It was the same photograph.

But now there was another change.

The boy standing beside the villagers was no longer alone.

The woman was standing behind him.

Her hand rested gently on his shoulder.

Ren's stomach turned.

"That's me."

The old man nodded.

"Yes."

"When was this taken?"

"Forty-three years ago."

Ren stared at him.

"That's impossible."

"Exactly."

The old man turned the photograph over.

There was writing on the back.

A date.

August 17, 1983.

And beneath it:

REN.

Ren's face went pale.

"My name wasn't even common then."

His grandmother suddenly looked away.

Ren noticed.

"Grandma?"

She said nothing.

"Why does the photograph have my name?"

Still nothing.

The old man answered for her.

"Because the photograph wasn't taken forty-three years ago."

Ren looked at him.

"It was taken tomorrow."

Silence.

The shrine bell moved gently in the wind.

Ren stared at the photograph again.

Then he noticed something else.

The woman in the picture wasn't looking at the camera.

She was looking directly at him.

The old man whispered:

"That's the reason we don't photograph her."

Ren swallowed.

"Why?"

"Because every photograph she appears in shows something that hasn't happened yet."

Ren looked at the date again.

August 17, 1983.

Then he checked his phone.

Today's date was August 16, 2026.

The photograph wasn't showing the past.

It was showing the future.

And tomorrow's date was---

August 17, 2026.

Ren slowly looked up.

"What's going to happen tomorrow?"

The old man didn't answer.

His grandmother did.

Her voice barely came out.

"You're going to disappear."

A sound came from the forest.

Po...

Ren froze.

His grandmother grabbed his hand.

The old man looked toward the trees.

Po... po...

Then the shrine lights went out.

In complete darkness, a woman's voice whispered from somewhere behind Ren:

"Ren."

He turned.

And this time...

he saw her face.
