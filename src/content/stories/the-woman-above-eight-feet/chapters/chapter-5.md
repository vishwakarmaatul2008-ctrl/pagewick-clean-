# Chapter 5 – The House

Ren couldn't move.

The shrine was completely dark.

He could hear his grandmother breathing beside him.

The old shopkeeper whispered:

"Don't turn around."

Ren's eyes remained fixed on the darkness ahead.

"But I already saw her."

"No," the old man said. "You saw what she wanted you to see."

A cold breeze passed behind Ren.

Then came the sound.

Po... po... po...

This time it wasn't outside.

It was right behind him.

Ren squeezed his eyes shut.

His grandmother grabbed his hand.

"Whatever you hear, don't answer."

A woman's voice whispered:

"Ren."

He said nothing.

"Ren..."

He stayed silent.

Then the voice changed.

It became his mother's voice.

"Ren, it's me."

His heart jumped.

His mother was hundreds of kilometers away.

The voice continued.

"Please turn around."

Ren's grandmother suddenly pulled him toward the shrine entrance.

"NOW."

They ran.

The old shopkeeper followed them, carrying the photograph.

None of them looked behind.

---

They reached Ren's grandmother's house just before sunrise.

The sky was beginning to turn gray.

Ren was exhausted.

But there was one thing he couldn't stop thinking about.

The locked room.

He looked at his grandmother.

"Everything started after I came here."

She didn't answer.

"That room..."

Her silence was enough.

Ren walked toward the back hallway.

His grandmother followed.

"Ren, don't."

He stopped in front of the locked door.

"What's inside?"

His grandmother looked at the floor.

"Your grandfather's room."

Ren stared at her.

"You said it was empty."

"I lied."

She reached into her pocket and pulled out a key.

Then she handed it to him.

"Your grandfather disappeared forty-three years ago."

Ren's fingers tightened around the key.

"The same year as the photograph?"

She nodded.

"He was the first person in our family to see her."

Ren unlocked the door.

Click.

The room smelled of dust and old wood.

A desk sat beneath the window.

On it were dozens of photographs.

Ren picked up the first one.

His grandfather.

The second.

His grandfather again.

The third---

The woman.

Always standing somewhere in the background.

Sometimes behind a tree.

Sometimes at the end of a road.

Sometimes inside a crowd.

Always watching.

Ren picked up another photograph.

His hands began to shake.

It showed his grandfather standing inside this exact room.

And behind him...

was the woman.

But there was something written on the photograph.

SHE DOESN'T FOLLOW PEOPLE.

Ren turned it over.

On the back:

SHE FOLLOWS THE HOUSE.

He looked at his grandmother.

"Why?"

She finally told him the truth.

"This house was built on the place where she died."

Ren stared at her.

"Who was she?"

"We don't know."

"Then how do you know she died here?"

"Because your great-grandfather found her body."

Ren looked around the room.

"What happened?"

His grandmother's eyes filled with tears.

"He buried her."

A long silence followed.

"Where?"

His grandmother pointed toward the window.

"Under the house."

Ren slowly stepped toward it.

The floorboards beneath his feet creaked.

Then he noticed something carved into the wooden wall.

A sentence.

Old.

Deep.

Almost hidden beneath layers of paint.

SHE WAS NEVER BURIED ALONE.

Ren turned around.

"Grandma..."

She looked terrified.

Before he could ask anything else---

Po... po... po...

The sound came from beneath them.

Not outside.

Not from the hallway.

From underneath the floor.

The wooden boards trembled.

Once.

Twice.

Then something knocked from below.

Tok.

Ren stepped backward.

His grandmother whispered:

"Don't open it."

Another knock.

Tok.

Then a third.

Tok.

The floorboard beneath Ren's feet slowly began to rise.

And from the darkness underneath came a woman's voice.

"Ren..."

This time, she wasn't calling from outside.

She was calling from inside the house.
