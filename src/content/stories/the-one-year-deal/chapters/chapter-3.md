# Chapter 3 – The Unexpected Candidate

"Come on, get her ready," Grandmother said.

Elena looked at her in confusion.

"Ready for what?"

"The party."

"But I was hired as a maid."

Grandmother smiled.

"You are not getting set up with some random man. It is my Damien."

She took out her phone and showed Elena a photograph.

"Look at him. He is very smart and good-looking. I know you can handle him."

Elena looked at the photograph for a moment before looking away.

"But I don't think I can handle him."

Grandmother laughed.

"Come on. If you can handle me, then you can definitely handle Damien."

Elena still looked uncertain.

"Pick whatever you want."

She looked around the enormous dressing room. Expensive dresses filled the racks, and jewelry sparkled beneath the lights.

Elena had never seen so many expensive and fancy dresses before.

"I don't know anything about these things. I'm happy with whatever you want to give me."

Grandmother smiled.

"Bring the most expensive dresses for her."

The staff immediately began selecting dresses.

Elena looked at them nervously.

"That's really not necessary."

Grandmother ignored her.

Then she suddenly remembered something.

"By the way, do you know how to swim?"

Elena shook her head.

"No."

Grandmother's eyes widened.

"Then why did you jump into the pool to save me?"

Elena answered calmly.

"I knew I couldn't swim."

"Then why?"

"But I knew I could save you."

Grandmother stared at her.

Her eyes became wide and bright.

Before Elena could say anything else, the staff brought a beautiful peach-colored ball gown.

Grandmother immediately pointed toward it.

"Get her into that."

Elena stared at the dress.

"That's too expensive."

"Do it fast!"

Grandmother turned toward the staff.

"And bring jewelry."

She looked at the jewelry collection.

"Bring the one with the biggest diamond."

Elena's eyes widened.

---

Meanwhile, downstairs, Damien was already uncomfortable.

The entire ballroom was filled with young women from powerful and wealthy families.

And almost every few minutes, another girl approached him.

"Damien, have you decided?"

"You should consider me."

"Our families have known each other for years."

"I think we would make a perfect couple."

Damien was becoming increasingly irritated.

He stood quietly with a glass in his hand, trying to escape the attention.

Then his friends approached him.

One of them smiled.

"Oh, man. You are lucky."

Damien looked at him.

"Lucky?"

"You've got so many girls to choose from."

Another friend laughed.

"Tell us. Did you choose a hottie or a baddie for yourself?"

Damien looked at him coldly.

"Are you here to irritate me more?"

His friend laughed.

"Nope."

He looked around at the women.

"If you don't like any of them, you can tell us. At least we'll get a chance to choose one."

Damien sighed.

"You two are unbelievable."

---

At the same time, Vivienne was sitting with her group of friends.

She was dressed elegantly, just like the other wealthy candidates.

One of the girls looked at her and smiled.

"Vivienne, you and Damien look good together."

Vivienne smiled confidently.

"Yeah. I know."

Another girl looked toward Damien.

"But there are other girls here too. What if Damien likes someone else? What are you going to do?"

Vivienne's expression changed.

She lifted her wine glass and suddenly threw the wine at the girl.

The girl gasped.

"Vivienne!"

"He is only mine."

The girls stared at her.

"But you were dating that other businessman. What about him?"

"I broke up with him."

"When?"

"As soon as I heard that Damien was looking for a fiancée."

Vivienne calmly picked up another glass.

"I don't need him anymore."

One of the girls looked at her.

"So you broke up with him because Damien is a better opportunity?"

Vivienne laughed.

"It was just an upgrade."

Another girl smiled teasingly.

"If you get someone better than Damien, you'll leave Damien too."

Vivienne laughed.

"Who knows?"

She took another sip of wine.

---

Upstairs, Elena was finally ready.

She stood in front of the mirror.

For several seconds, she simply stared at her reflection.

She was wearing the peach-colored ball gown Grandmother had chosen.

Her long dark-brown hair had been styled into soft curly waves that fell over her shoulders.

A beautiful diamond necklace rested around her neck.

The simple girl who had arrived at the mansion in a sleeveless top and mini skirt now looked completely different.

The staff were stunned.

Elena touched the necklace nervously.

"This is too much."

The head maid smiled.

"You look beautiful."

Elena looked at herself again.

"I don't feel like myself."

Then Grandmother entered.

She stopped when she saw Elena.

A huge smile appeared on her face.

"I knew it."

Elena turned toward her.

"What?"

Grandmother looked at her proudly.

"I found a treasure from the water."

The staff laughed.

Elena covered her face.

"Please don't call me that."

Grandmother laughed.

"Come on. The party has already started."

---

The ballroom was already crowded when Grandmother entered.

But she wasn't alone.

Elena walked beside her.

The moment they entered, conversations began to fade.

People turned toward them.

The wealthy guests stared.

The candidates stared.

Damien's friends stared.

Vivienne and her friends stared.

Everyone wanted to know the same thing.

Who was this girl walking beside Madam Moretti?

Whispers quickly spread throughout the hall.

"Who is she?"

"I've never seen her before."

"Was she invited?"

"Why is she walking with Madam Moretti?"

Damien's friends looked at him.

One of them leaned closer.

"I think your grandmother has already chosen your fiancée."

Damien frowned.

"What?"

His friend pointed toward Elena.

"Look at her."

Damien looked at Elena.

He had never seen this girl before.

"I don't know her."

His friend laughed.

"Maybe your grandmother knows better than you."

Damien didn't answer.

His eyes remained on Elena for a moment.

---

"Damien."

Grandmother called him.

His friends immediately started teasing him.

"Go."

"Your grandmother is calling you."

"Go meet your fiancée."

Damien gave them an irritated look before walking toward his grandmother.

Grandmother smiled.

"Look how handsome you look today."

Damien smiled.

"Thank you."

Then Grandmother turned toward Elena.

"Look at her. She is my special guest."

Elena gave Damien a nervous smile.

"Hi."

Damien extended his hand.

"My name is Damien Moretti."

Elena hesitated before taking his hand.

"My name is Elena Rossi."

She looked down almost immediately.

Grandmother smiled.

"Elena, come sit here. I'll just come back in two minutes."

She turned toward Damien.

"You come with me."

Damien sighed.

"Grandmother..."

"Come."

She walked away with him.

Elena remained behind.

She looked around the ballroom.

Everything was new to her.

The lights.

The music.

The expensive decorations.

The wealthy people.

She had never experienced a party like this before.

Suddenly Damien's friends approached her.

"So, Elena..."

She looked at them.

"Do you like Damien?"

Elena's eyes widened.

"What?"

"What does your family do?"

"What's your name?"

"How did you get this invitation?"

Elena tried to answer them.

"My name is Elena Rossi."

She paused.

"I sell cupcakes and pastries."

They looked surprised.

"You sell cupcakes?"

"Yes."

"How did you get invited to this party?"

Elena looked toward Grandmother.

"She invited me."

"Why?"

Elena answered honestly.

"I saved her."

His friends looked at each other.

---

Meanwhile, Grandmother had taken Damien to a quieter part of the ballroom.

"Do you like anyone from the party?"

Damien answered immediately.

"No."

Grandmother smiled.

"I knew it."

Damien looked at her.

"And I have found someone for you."

Damien frowned.

"I knew it. Who is she?"

Grandmother laughed.

"I didn't tell you anything about her, and I certainly haven't finalized anything."

"Then what do you mean?"

"I gave her a chance."

Damien looked toward Elena.

"Do you like her?"

"Grandmother, tell me the truth clearly."

She smiled.

"Okay. Yes. I think she is perfect for you."

Damien frowned.

"What do you mean?"

Grandmother finally told him what had happened.

"Elena was walking through the backyard and saw me near a group of girls."

Damien listened carefully.

"One of those girls bumped into me and I fell into the pool."

Damien's expression changed.

"What happened after that?"

"They didn't help me."

"Why?"

"They were worried about their expensive dresses and makeup."

Damien's face became serious.

"And Elena?"

"She jumped into the pool."

Damien stared at her.

"She doesn't know how to swim."

"I know."

"Then how did she save you?"

Grandmother smiled.

"She struggled, but she brought me to the edge."

Damien became angry.

"Grandmother, are you mad? You were drowning. Why didn't you call me?"

"I didn't have time."

"You need to take care of yourself."

Grandmother smiled.

"Don't worry. That girl came like a mermaid and saved me."

Damien stared at her.

"First you tell me she doesn't know how to swim, and now you're telling me she saved you like a mermaid?"

Grandmother laughed.

"I mean she looked like a mermaid in that moment."

Damien shook his head.

"Stop doing these things and take care of yourself."

"I will."

Grandmother smiled.

"If you start dating this girl."

Damien looked at her.

"She isn't rich," Grandmother continued. "She doesn't have a family. She sells cupcakes on the street."

Damien remained silent.

"I have her full background information."

He looked toward Elena.

"Okay."

Grandmother raised an eyebrow.

"I'll talk to her first."

Grandmother smiled.

"Good."

---

Grandmother returned to Elena.

"Elena."

Elena looked at her.

"My grandson wants to talk with you alone."

Elena immediately became nervous.

"Me?"

"Yes."

Damien stood nearby.

Elena followed him to a quieter part of the ballroom.

Damien looked at her.

"The first thing I want to ask is about my grandmother."

Elena nodded.

"She was walking through the backyard. I saw her with a group of girls, and then one of the girls accidentally pushed her into the pool."

Damien listened.

"So you jumped in?"

"Yes."

"You didn't know how to swim."

"No."

"Then why did you do it?"

Elena looked down.

"She was drowning."

She paused.

"I knew I couldn't swim, but I knew I had more energy compared to an old lady who was drowning."

Damien remained silent.

"I pushed myself toward the edge and brought her there."

She continued looking down.

Damien asked about her work.

"I sell cupcakes and pastries on the street."

"And that's what you do professionally?"

"Yes."

There was a short silence.

Elena looked uncomfortable.

"If you're done, can I go now?"

Damien nodded.

"Yes."

"Thank you."

Elena walked away.

Throughout the entire conversation, she hadn't looked into Damien's eyes even once for more than a moment.

She returned to Grandmother.

"I think I should go home."

Grandmother looked at her.

"Why?"

"I have work."

"I think you have to stay here."

Elena became confused.

"But why?"

Grandmother smiled.

"I think my grandson likes you."

Elena's eyes widened.

"What?"

Grandmother laughed.

"Don't worry. You can do whatever you want."

Elena relaxed slightly.

"But you're in a relationship with my grandson."

Elena froze.

"What?"

"You don't have to marry him."

Elena looked confused.

"If you don't like him, you can break up with him."

"That sounds better."

"But..."

Grandmother smiled.

"You have to stay in the relationship for one year."

Elena stared at her.

"One year?"

"Yes."

Elena looked toward Damien.

She had come to the Moretti mansion expecting to work as a maid.

She never imagined that before the night was over, she would be asked to become the girlfriend of the man she had just met.

And she had no idea what the next year would bring.
