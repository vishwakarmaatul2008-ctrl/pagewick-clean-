# The First Reflection

The mansion was nothing like any of them had imagined.

Moonlight poured through the cracked glass windows, illuminating polished marble floors and grand staircases draped in dust. Although the exterior looked abandoned and ready to collapse, the inside still carried traces of the luxury it once possessed.

Magnus let out a low whistle.

"Wow... this place is actually beautiful."

He slowly turned in a circle, admiring the enormous chandeliers hanging above them.

"It looks more like a palace than a haunted mansion."

Jackson nodded in agreement.

"Honestly, the inside is in much better condition than the outside."

Iris crossed her arms.

"Are you two done inspecting the architecture?"

"Can we leave now?"

"I think we've collected enough information."

Ace quickly agreed.

"Yeah. We've already completed the dare."

Nick glanced toward the entrance.

"I think we should head back."

Jackson looked at Magnus.

"Come on, bro."

"There's nothing here."

"It's just an old mansion. Maybe someday the government will restore it."

He smiled at Iris.

"And she's already scared."

"We can always come back during the daytime."

Magnus folded his arms.

"Leave already?"

"We've only been here for two minutes."

A grin slowly spread across his face.

"I've got a better idea."

Irene sighed.

"I already don't like that sentence."

Magnus ignored her.

"We split into groups."

Immediately, everyone began protesting.

"No."

Irene spoke before anyone else could.

"We're not exploring this place."

"And we're definitely not splitting up."

Magnus shrugged.

"Relax."

"We're not stealing anything."

"We're just looking around."

"Then we leave."

Before Irene could argue again, Magnus continued.

"Jackson and Iris take the upper floor."

"Nick, Ace and I will check the basement."

"And Tom..."

He looked toward Irene with a mischievous smile.

"You'll explore the ground floor with Irene."

Irene frowned.

"Why me?"

Magnus laughed.

"Maybe spending time with you will finally make him brave."

Tom smiled awkwardly.

"I don't mind."

Irene rolled her eyes.

Jackson turned toward Iris.

"If you're uncomfortable..."

"I can take you back to the car."

She shook her head.

"No."

"If you're staying..."

"I'm staying."

Magnus clapped his hands.

"Perfect."

"If anyone finds something interesting or needs help..."

"Call everyone else."

One by one, the groups separated.

---

The mansion became strangely quiet.

Only distant footsteps echoed through the endless corridors.

Irene walked slowly along the ground floor while Tom followed a few steps behind.

The silence between them felt heavier than the walls surrounding them.

Tom finally spoke.

"Are you upset because you're stuck with me?"

Irene looked ahead.

"No."

"I just don't like this place."

She paused.

"The air feels..."

"...heavy."

Tom looked around nervously.

"Then why did you come?"

She smiled faintly.

"Because everyone else came."

"And Iris looked worried."

"I didn't want to leave her."

She glanced at him.

"What about you?"

Tom scratched the back of his neck.

"You."

"I came because of you."

He laughed softly.

"I really like you."

Irene sighed.

"You've said that to other girls too."

"You dated them."

"Lied to them."

"Then broke up with them."

Tom immediately shook his head.

"But you're different."

"I'd protect you from anything."

The words had barely left his mouth...

A loud crash echoed somewhere behind them.

Tom jumped.

"What was that?!"

Without even looking back—

He ran.

Irene blinked in disbelief.

"Tom!"

She turned toward the source of the noise.

A family of rats scattered across the hallway before disappearing into broken walls.

"...Seriously?"

She let out a long sigh.

"I hope I find him before he gets himself into trouble."

---

Tom kept running until he reached another hallway.

His heart pounded inside his chest.

He looked around desperately.

"Irene?"

"Where are you?"

He swallowed.

"Why didn't she run with me?"

The silence answered him.

Then—

"Tom..."

His eyes widened.

"Irene?"

"Tom..."

Her voice came again.

Soft.

Gentle.

Coming from one of the nearby rooms.

Relief washed over him.

"I finally found you."

He stepped inside.

Darkness filled the room.

Broken furniture covered the floor.

Only one tall object stood hidden behind an old curtain.

"Irene?"

"Where are you?"

"We should stay here until everyone comes back."

The voice answered again.

"Tom..."

"Come closer."

He smiled nervously.

"Are you hiding behind the curtain?"

He forced himself to laugh.

"I'm not that much of a coward."

Slowly...

He reached for the curtain.

And pulled it aside.

There was no Irene.

Only an enormous mirror.

Its surface shimmered unnaturally.

Before Tom could react—

A girl's reflection smiled back at him.

But it wasn't his reflection.

Long strands of hair, sharp glass-like teeth, eyes that glowed like cracked crystal...

The girl inside the mirror tilted her head.

Her lips curled into a smile.

Then...

A hand emerged from the mirror.

It wrapped around Tom's throat.

His smile disappeared instantly.

"W-What..."

CRACK.

Pain exploded through his neck.

Tom screamed.

He clawed desperately at the icy fingers tightening around him.

"No...!"

The mirror rippled like water.

The ghost pulled him forward.

His body slowly disappeared into the reflection.

His screams echoed through the mansion...

Then...

Silence.

The mirror became perfectly still once again.

As though nothing had ever happened.

Tom was the first to explore the mansion but never to leave.
