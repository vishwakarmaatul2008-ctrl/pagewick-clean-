# The Last Survivor

The mansion had fallen silent.

Not peaceful.

Silent enough for Irene to hear her own heartbeat.

"Tom?"

She walked through the endless hallway, her voice trembling.

"Jackson!"

"Iris!"

"Nick!"

"Ace!"

"Magnus!"

No answer.

Only the sound of the cold wind slipping through broken windows.

She pulled out her phone and switched on the flashlight.

Battery: 4%

"...Seriously?"

The weak beam barely lit the hallway.

She hurried forward, opening room after room.

Nothing.

No friends.

No footsteps.

Then—

"Irene..."

Jackson's voice echoed upstairs.

Relief filled her face.

"Jackson?"

She took one step toward the staircase.

"Irene..."

This time the voice came from behind.

She froze.

Slowly...

She turned around.

Nothing.

The hallway was empty.

Her heartbeat quickened.

"...Who's there?"

Silence.

Her phone suddenly died.

Darkness swallowed the mansion.

"No..."

She searched blindly until her hand brushed against an old wooden cabinet.

Inside rested an antique oil lamp.

Beside it lay a dusty bottle of oil.

After several tries...

The wick finally caught fire.

A warm orange glow pushed back the darkness.

"That's better..."

Holding the lamp tightly, she continued searching.

---

Drops of blood led toward a half-open room.

"Tom?"

She slowly pushed the door open.

The room was empty.

Then...

She noticed a hand.

It was sticking halfway out of a mirror.

Her breathing stopped.

She walked closer.

Tom.

Half of his body had disappeared inside the glass.

His neck was twisted.

His terrified eyes stared into nothing.

Irene covered her mouth.

"No..."

Tears rolled down her face.

"I'm sorry..."

She gently closed his eyes before walking away.

There was nothing she could do.

---

On the second floor...

She found Jackson and Iris.

Jackson still held Iris in his arms.

Their bodies rested against the wall.

Neither had let go.

Irene slowly knelt beside them.

She couldn't speak.

Only tears fell onto the dusty floor.

"You made it back to each other..."

She whispered.

"...I'm just sorry it ended like this."

She stood.

She still refused to believe everyone was gone.

---

The basement was colder.

Blood covered the stone floor.

Ace lay near a shattered mirror.

Nick remained tied before another.

His glasses were missing.

Several meters away...

Magnus' bloody footprints ended near the basement door.

Broken lenses lay nearby.

Irene stared silently.

"What happened here..."

No answer came.

Only silence.

Then...

Every mirror rippled at once.

A young girl appeared inside them.

Crystal hair.

Glass eyes.

A smile filled with razor-sharp teeth.

The girl slowly tilted her head.

"Irene..."

She spoke.

Using Iris' voice.

"Help me..."

Irene's eyes widened.

"...You're not Iris."

The smile grew wider.

The ghost had been found.

Without warning—

Hundreds of razor-sharp shards burst from the mirrors.

Irene ducked behind a pillar.

Glass exploded around her.

She ran.

The ghost followed through every reflection.

Windows.

Picture frames.

Mirrors.

She appeared everywhere.

Every time Irene escaped one reflection...

Another was already waiting.

---

Breathing heavily, Irene stumbled into the mansion's ballroom.

Mirrors covered every wall.

She was trapped.

The ghost disappeared.

Irene looked around desperately.

Then—

CRACK.

She looked down.

Inside the glass chimney of the oil lamp...

The ghost smiled back at her.

Crystal fingers pressed against the glass.

Tiny cracks spread across the lamp.

"So..."

"You can use this too..."

Instinct took over.

Irene threw the lamp across the room.

CRASH!

The glass shattered against a heavy curtain.

Burning oil splashed across the cloth.

Fire climbed upward.

The ghost stepped into another nearby mirror.

Still smiling.

Then...

Smoke filled the ballroom.

Black soot slowly settled across the nearest mirrors.

The ghost reached toward one.

Nothing.

She quickly turned toward another.

It too had become black.

Again...

Nothing.

The smile disappeared.

Irene stared.

"...You need reflections."

The ghost screamed.

Glass exploded through the ballroom.

Instead of aiming at Irene...

The ghost aimed at the burning curtains.

Razor-sharp shards sliced the curtains into dozens of pieces before they could cover every mirror.

The ghost had learned Irene's plan.

One clean mirror remained.

The ghost emerged from it instantly.

A long shard of crystal slashed across Irene's shoulder.

"Aah!"

Another cut her leg.

She fell hard onto the floor.

Blood stained her sleeve.

The ghost slowly walked toward her through the mirror.

For the first time...

She had nowhere left to run.

Irene looked around.

Another curtain.

Still untouched.

She crawled toward it.

The ghost launched another storm of glass.

Several shards struck Irene's arm and back.

Ignoring the pain, Irene grabbed the curtain and pulled with all her strength.

The fabric fell into the spreading fire.

Flames raced across it.

Using the burning cloth, Irene dragged it across the nearest mirrors as she stumbled toward the exit.

Smoke spread faster.

One mirror disappeared beneath soot.

Then another.

Then another.

The ghost desperately moved from reflection to reflection.

Each time...

Another mirror went black.

Soon...

Only one mirror remained clean.

It stood beside the ballroom door.

The ghost appeared inside it one final time.

Their eyes met.

The ghost smiled.

"Irene..."

She whispered again in Iris' voice.

"Don't leave me..."

Irene closed her eyes.

For a brief moment...

She wanted to believe it.

Then she remembered.

Her friends were gone.

She opened her eyes.

Without answering...

She threw the last burning curtain across the mirror.

The reflection vanished beneath smoke.

The ghost disappeared.

Not dead.

Only trapped.

The mansion shook violently.

The burning ceiling began collapsing.

Glass rained from above.

Irene limped toward the entrance as fire spread through every corridor.

She passed each room one last time.

Tom.

Jackson.

Iris.

Ace.

Nick.

Magnus.

"I'm sorry..."

She whispered.

Then she ran.

The front door burst open.

She threw herself outside just as burning beams crashed behind her.

The mansion burned through the rest of the night.

Deep inside...

Beyond the smoke...

One untouched mirror remained hidden within a collapsed room.

Slowly...

The Mirror Ghost appeared inside it.

Her crystal smile returned.

She watched the flames dance across the broken glass.

She couldn't leave.

But she had never needed to.

The mansion was still hers.

---

Weeks later...

The police recovered the bodies of the six students.

They blamed the tragedy on the fire and the collapsing building.

Only Irene survived.

Long scars crossed her shoulder, arms, and leg.

No one believed her story.

Eventually...

The mansion was abandoned once again.

Years later...

Travelers still spoke about the old mansion hidden beyond the forest.

Some claimed they saw a girl standing inside its broken windows.

Others heard voices calling their names.

No one who believed the stories ever entered.

And Irene...

Never looked into a mirror longer than necessary.

Because she knew...

Some reflections were waiting for someone to look back.
