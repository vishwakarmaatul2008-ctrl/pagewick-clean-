# The Price of Survival

The basement was colder than the rest of the mansion.

Unlike the grand halls above, these corridors were narrow, damp, and swallowed by darkness.

Dust covered every corner, while enormous mirrors leaned against the stone walls like forgotten relics.

Ace rubbed his arms.

"I don't like this place."

Nick adjusted his glasses, studying the surroundings.

"I think whoever owned this mansion cared deeply about appearance."

Ace looked at him.

"What makes you say that?"

Nick pointed toward the walls.

"Everywhere we go..."

"There are mirrors."

"And glass."

"I think the owner enjoyed looking at their own reflection."

Magnus sighed loudly.

"Can you stop analyzing everything?"

"We came here to find something interesting."

As if the mansion had heard him...

A deafening crash echoed through the basement.

The sound bounced off every wall.

Nick jumped.

"W-What was that?"

Ace swallowed.

"It was way too loud."

Magnus forced a laugh.

"Probably a window."

Ace stared at him.

"Are you serious?"

"We're underground."

"How would the wind reach the basement?"

Nick slowly nodded.

"Ace is right."

"I think we should leave."

Magnus shrugged.

"Fine."

"You two walk in front."

"I'll watch behind us."

The three slowly walked toward the staircase.

Then—

A scream echoed behind them.

They turned.

A girl stood inside one of the mirrors.

Crystal hair.

Glass eyes.

A smile filled with razor-sharp teeth.

Before anyone could react—

Hundreds of glass shards exploded toward them.

"RUN!"

Nick shouted.

The three sprinted through the basement as glass shattered around them.

Nick spotted an old wooden storage closet.

"There!"

"Hide!"

He threw open the door.

One by one they rushed toward it.

Nick climbed inside first.

He turned, holding the door open.

"Ace!"

"Hurry!"

Ace reached the doorway.

Just as he grabbed the handle—

Magnus shoved him backward.

Ace stumbled.

"M-Magnus?"

Magnus forced his way inside.

Then slammed the closet door shut.

BANG.

Ace pounded desperately from outside.

"Magnus!"

"Open the door!"

"Please!"

The screams continued.

Then—

Glass shattered.

Ace cried out in agony.

Nick covered his mouth.

Unable to watch.

Unable to look away.

The ghost's crystal arm emerged from a nearby mirror.

It pierced Ace's chest.

Another mirror opened beneath his feet like dark water.

His body slowly disappeared inside.

His screams echoed through the basement.

Then...

Silence.

Nothing remained except blood.

Magnus leaned against the closet door, breathing heavily.

"The ghost..."

"Got him."

Nick couldn't answer.

He simply nodded.

---

Several minutes passed.

The silence outside convinced them to leave the closet.

They stepped carefully into the hallway.

Ace was gone.

Only streaks of blood remained...

Leading toward a mirror.

Nick stared at it.

"So that's how she kills."

Magnus looked at him.

"What?"

Nick spoke quietly.

"She attacks through reflections."

"Mirrors."

"Glass."

"If we avoid reflections..."

"We might survive."

He slowly turned toward Magnus.

"Why did you push Ace?"

Magnus didn't hesitate.

"He was slowing me down."

Nick stared at him in disbelief.

"You sacrificed your own friend."

"So?"

Magnus shrugged.

"I survived."

Nick's voice became cold.

"You're disgusting."

"You think strength alone will get you out."

"But without my brain..."

"You won't survive this mansion."

"I'm leaving."

He turned away.

Magnus grabbed his shoulder.

"What did you say?"

Nick pulled free.

"You heard me."

The punch came without warning.

CRACK.

Nick fell to the floor.

His glasses slid across the stone.

Magnus picked them up.

"You think these glasses make you smarter?"

He laughed.

"I'll keep them."

He shoved Nick against a massive mirror.

Then tore a length of old rope from nearby shelves and tied Nick tightly.

"I'd love to beat you some more..."

He smirked.

"But I think..."

He looked toward the mirror.

"...I'll let her finish the job."

Magnus walked away.

Nick struggled desperately.

Without his glasses...

Everything beyond a few feet became a blur.

Then...

The mirror rippled.

The ghost slowly appeared behind him.

Nick closed his eyes.

"So..."

"My theory..."

"Was right."

A crystal hand reached through the mirror.

It wrapped around his shoulders.

Without a scream...

Nick disappeared into the reflection.

The mirror became still once more.

---

Magnus walked confidently toward the basement exit.

"Worthless nerd."

He adjusted Nick's glasses.

"Now I'm the smart one."

He reached the heavy wooden door leading upstairs.

Locked.

He frowned.

"No problem."

"There aren't any mirrors here."

"If I break the door..."

"I escape."

He stepped back.

Raised his foot.

Then suddenly froze.

A reflection.

Tiny.

Barely visible.

Inside Nick's glasses.

The ghost was smiling directly behind him.

His eyes widened.

"No..."

Too late.

Two enormous crystal spikes burst from the reflection.

They pierced both of his eyes.

Magnus screamed in agony.

He ripped the glasses from his face and stumbled backward.

The basement door slowly creaked open.

Beyond it...

Dozens of mirrors lined the hallway.

Every reflection contained the same smiling girl.

Hundreds of crystal hands emerged together.

They seized Magnus.

"No!"

He fought wildly.

Kicked.

Punched.

Screamed.

But the mirrors pulled him deeper.

His voice echoed through the basement...

Until the reflections swallowed him whole.

The hallway fell silent once again.

The basement...

Belonged only to the mirrors.
