# Reflections

The second floor was eerily quiet.

Unlike the grand hall downstairs, this floor was lined with long corridors, elegant mirrors, and countless closed doors.

Every step echoed through the empty mansion.

Iris folded her arms as she walked beside Jackson.

"I still don't understand you."

Jackson looked at her.

"What do you mean?"

"You never listen to me."

"I told you not to come here."

"You still came."

Jackson smiled faintly.

"I told you..."

"I'll always stay by your side."

Iris stopped walking.

"That's not the point."

"I don't need you to stand beside me."

"I need you to listen."

Jackson sighed.

"I always listen."

"But sometimes..."

He hesitated.

"You never listen to me either."

Iris frowned.

"What are you talking about?"

"You don't like it when I spend time with Magnus or my other friends."

"But I've never stopped you from spending time with Irene or your other friends."

Silence.

Iris looked away.

"So you've been keeping an eye on me?"

Jackson shook his head.

"No."

"I've just been paying attention."

"I wish you'd understand how I feel."

His voice was calm.

"I've never wanted to control you."

"I just wanted you to trust me."

Tears slowly filled Iris's eyes.

Without thinking...

She slapped him across the cheek.

The sound echoed through the empty hallway.

Jackson didn't move.

He simply looked at her.

Iris immediately regretted it.

Unable to face him...

She turned and ran.

"Iris!"

Jackson reached out, but she had already disappeared around the corner.

---

Iris finally stopped running.

She leaned against the cold wall, trying to catch her breath.

"What did I just do..."

She wiped away her tears.

"Maybe..."

"I never really tried to understand him."

"And maybe..."

"He never understood me either."

She took a deep breath.

"I should go back."

"I'm going to apologize."

Suddenly...

A loud noise echoed somewhere behind her.

She froze.

"Jackson?"

She quickly turned around.

A familiar figure stood at the end of the hallway.

Jackson.

Relief washed over her.

She ran toward him and wrapped her arms around him.

"I'm sorry."

"I shouldn't have run away."

"I shouldn't have slapped you."

She buried her face against his chest.

"You found me so quickly."

Jackson smiled softly.

"I knew..."

"You didn't really want to leave."

Iris laughed through her tears.

"So..."

"You were the one who made that noise?"

Jackson nodded.

"It was just a rat."

The tension between them finally disappeared.

Iris gently kissed him on the cheek.

"I'm sorry."

"I never gave you the time or love you deserved."

Jackson smiled.

"And I'm sorry too."

"I should've listened to you instead of Magnus."

"Let's leave."

"Right now."

Neither of them noticed...

The enormous mirror behind them.

Its reflection rippled like water.

Something inside...

Had been listening to every word.

Its surface trembled.

Not from wind.

Not from age.

The reflection itself...

Moved.

A sharp cracking sound suddenly echoed across the hallway.

CRACK!

The mirror exploded.

Hundreds of razor-sharp glass shards shot toward them like bullets.

"IRIS!"

Jackson reacted instantly.

He wrapped both arms around her and threw them sideways.

Glass shattered against the walls behind them.

Several pieces sliced across Jackson's shoulder and arm.

Another shard grazed Iris's cheek.

They rolled across the dusty floor.

Jackson quickly pushed himself to his feet.

"What the hell was that?"

Before either of them could answer...

The broken mirror slowly repaired itself.

Every shattered piece floated through the air.

One by one...

They returned to the empty frame.

The cracks disappeared.

The reflection became perfectly smooth again.

Then...

She appeared.

A young girl stood inside the mirror.

Her long hair shimmered like transparent glass.

Crystal-like eyes stared without blinking.

Her fingernails were as sharp as broken mirrors.

When she smiled...

Rows of jagged glass teeth reflected the moonlight.

Neither of them spoke.

Neither of them moved.

The girl slowly tilted her head.

A smile spread across her face.

Then—

Her arm reached through the mirror.

Not around it.

Through it.

As if the glass were water.

"What..."

Jackson whispered.

The ghost suddenly flicked her wrist.

Dozens of razor-sharp glass fragments burst from the mirror again.

Jackson grabbed Iris and pulled her behind a stone pillar.

The glass shattered against the marble walls.

"This isn't possible..."

Iris's voice trembled.

Jackson looked toward the mirror.

"Stay away from it."

"Don't get anywhere near the mirrors."

The ghost smiled again.

Another shard formed inside the mirror.

This one wasn't small.

It was the size of a hunting knife.

Without warning...

She launched it.

Jackson tried to dodge.

Too late.

The glass pierced straight through his thigh.

He cried out and collapsed onto one knee.

"Jackson!"

Iris rushed toward him.

"No!"

Jackson shouted.

"Stay back!"

But she ignored him.

The ghost raised both hands.

This time...

Glass rained from every direction.

The hallway became a storm of shattered mirrors.

Jackson shielded Iris with his own body as pieces tore through his jacket.

Blood stained the floor beneath them.

The nightmare...

Had only just begun.

Jackson gritted his teeth as another wave of glass exploded from the mirror.

"IRIS!"

He threw himself in front of her.

The razor-sharp shards tore through his jacket and cut into his back and arms.

He refused to let a single piece reach her.

The ghost tilted her head.

Her crystal eyes never blinked.

She raised one hand again.

A spear of glass slowly formed inside the mirror.

With a flick of her wrist...

It shot through the hallway.

Jackson tried to move.

Too late.

The shard pierced straight through his left leg.

He collapsed onto one knee, letting out a cry of pain.

"Jackson!"

Iris rushed toward him despite everything he had said.

"I told you to stay away from the mirrors!" Jackson shouted.

"I'm not leaving you!"

Before Jackson could answer, dozens of glass needles burst from every mirror lining the hallway.

The walls...

The ceiling...

Even shattered picture frames.

Every reflection became a weapon.

The two desperately dodged as glass shattered around them.

Blood dripped onto the marble floor.

Suddenly—

A massive shard shot toward Iris.

She turned too late.

The jagged piece slammed through her hand and pinned it against the wall.

"AAAH!"

Iris screamed.

Her hand trembled as blood ran down the glass.

Jackson looked up.

"Iris!"

Ignoring the pain in his own leg, he grabbed the shard embedded in his thigh.

With a roar, he ripped it out.

Blood poured down his leg.

He stumbled...

Then forced himself to stand.

The ghost smiled.

Another spear formed.

This one flew faster.

It pierced Jackson's other leg.

His body collapsed again.

Every muscle screamed for him to stop.

Instead...

He clenched his fists.

"No..."

He forced himself upright once more.

"I won't let you touch her."

Using the last of his strength, Jackson charged toward the mirror.

The ghost calmly watched him.

A gigantic blade of crystal slowly emerged from the reflection.

It was as long as a sword.

Jackson never slowed down.

"GET AWAY FROM HER!"

The blade shot forward.

It drove deep into his stomach.

His body jerked violently.

For a brief moment...

Everything became silent.

Then...

Jackson screamed and threw his fist forward.

CRASH!

His punch struck the mirror with every ounce of strength he had left.

The entire hallway shook.

Cracks spread across the glass.

The ghost disappeared.

Every floating shard instantly fell to the floor.

Silence.

Iris pulled the glass from her hand and ran toward him.

"Jackson!"

He collapsed into her arms.

She gently lifted his head onto her lap.

His breathing had become weak.

"I... I'm sorry..." he whispered.

"I should've listened to you."

Tears streamed down Iris's face.

"No..."

"Don't talk."

"We're leaving together."

Jackson smiled faintly.

"There... might not be enough time."

He looked into her eyes.

"Iris..."

"I really loved you."

His voice almost disappeared.

"Did..."

"Did you ever love me too?"

Iris broke down completely.

She held his face with trembling hands.

"Yes."

"I love you."

"I've always loved you."

"I'm sorry..."

"I'm so sorry."

Jackson smiled.

For the first time that night...

He looked at peace.

Then...

CRACK...

A tiny sound echoed behind them.

Both slowly turned.

The broken mirror...

Was repairing itself.

Every shattered fragment rose into the air.

One by one...

They fused together.

The cracks vanished.

The reflection became perfect once again.

The Mirror Ghost stood inside.

Watching them.

Waiting.

She slowly lifted both hands.

The mirrors surrounding the hallway trembled.

Thousands of glittering fragments gathered high above them.

The pieces joined together...

Forming an enormous roof made entirely of glass.

Iris tightened her embrace around Jackson.

He weakly wrapped one arm around her.

Neither of them tried to run.

They simply held each other.

The ghost lowered her hand.

The glass ceiling crashed toward them.

At the last second...

The mirror didn't shatter.

It rippled like dark water.

Their bodies sank into it.

Swallowed whole.

The enormous mirror slammed against the marble floor with a deafening crash.

Silence returned to the hallway.

Only one thing remained.

A thin stream of blood slowly flowed from beneath the mirror...

Creeping across the white marble floor.

The mansion had claimed two more souls.
